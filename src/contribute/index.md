# Contribute

The project gets better when people push back on it, fix it, add to it, and spread it. Every level of involvement counts. Money is one option; it's not the most useful one.

## Engage

**Read** what's here. The site has [an overview of the party](/square-party/), info about the [fundamental representation problem](/representation/), the [assembly project example](/paul/campaign/empower/) (PA-3).

## Share

**Share** a link to someone and tell them what you think.

## Fund

The party doesn't have a treasury yet. Reach out to [paul@squareparty.org](mailto:paul@squareparty.org) if you want to contribute financially or help with financial logistics.  

## Contact

Email [paul@squareparty.org](mailto:paul@squareparty.org) to get in touch with a real person.
