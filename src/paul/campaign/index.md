# PA-3 Campaign

Paul is running as a write-in candidate for the U.S. House of Representatives in Pennsylvania's 3rd Congressional District. The campaign runs on Square Party terms: it's as much about the framework as the office.

The two tracks feed each other but don't block each other. Pledging a vote doesn't require reading the analysis. Reading the analysis doesn't require pledging a vote.

## Why a write-in

Pennsylvania's ballot-access rules for new parties and independents are demanding. The write-in path keeps the focus on the actual work rather than on the procedural challenge of getting on a ballot. It's also an honest reflection of where this stands: a project that reaches voters through write-in support is one that has earned that support on its substance, not on its access.

The closest precedent is Roosevelt's 1912 Bull Moose run, the most successful third-party presidential campaign in U.S. history, which still lost. A write-in House campaign in PA-3 isn't trying to be the exception that wins. It's trying to *change the conversation* about what representation could look like, and to use the cycle to put the framework in front of people who might pick it up after.
