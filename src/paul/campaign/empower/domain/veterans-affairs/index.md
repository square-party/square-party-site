# Veterans Affairs

<p class="domain-lead">Veterans Affairs is Title 38 architecture: the VA healthcare system (the largest integrated healthcare system in the U.S.), GI Bill and Post-9/11 education benefits, USERRA, veteran preference in employment, VBA disability and pension benefits. Treated as its own domain because Title 38 operates under a distinct legal regime from civilian counterparts even where the constituent's experience runs across both.</p>
