# Meet the Neighbors — Public Benefits & Safety Net

<p class="domain-lead">Composite household profiles for D12 are queued. The form mirrors the D9 Neighbors page: verified numbers from current law applied to documented PA-3 conditions, real neighborhoods with their statistical character intact, constructed people with no names and no claim to identifiable individuals. The profiles are devices for seeing what current policy produces for a household at a specific address — what the system delivers, and what it doesn't, after the 2025 OBBBA reshape and Pennsylvania's first state EITC took effect together.</p>

<aside class="construction-note">
<p><strong>Why this form.</strong> The structural finding is what's verified, not any individual story. A statement attributed to a real named person would be either fabricated or cherry-picked. The composite says: this is the typical experience of a household in this position. The "Composite" framing travels with each card.</p>
</aside>
