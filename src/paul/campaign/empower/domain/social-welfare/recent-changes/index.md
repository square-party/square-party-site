# Recent Changes — Public Benefits & Safety Net

<p class="domain-lead">D12's Material Changes log is queued. The verified file documents the most significant single-year set of changes to the federal safety net since 1996. The changelog will summarize, in cross-referenced entries with the sub-domains each materially affects: the OBBBA reshape (P.L. 119-21, July 4, 2025) of Medicaid (6-month redeterminations; 80-hour community-engagement reporting; cost-sharing up to $35 per service effective October 2028; provider-tax safe-harbor stepdown from 6% to 3.5% FY 2028–FY 2034) and SNAP (ABAWD age raised 18–54 to 18–64; parent-exemption tightened; SUA restricted; state administrative cost share rising from 50% to 75% in FY 2027); Pennsylvania's first state EITC enacted November 12, 2025 (Working Pennsylvanians Tax Credit at 10% of federal, up to $805); CBO scoring projections; CMS February 2026 baseline; and the procedural mechanisms that condition receipt at the household level.</p>
