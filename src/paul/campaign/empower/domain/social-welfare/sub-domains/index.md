# Sub-Domains within Public Benefits & Safety Net

<p class="domain-lead">Public benefits in PA-3 is not a single program. Seven sub-domains analyze the cash, health-coverage, nutrition, housing, disability-support, child-and-family, and elder-support architectures that determine household receipt. An eighth synthesizes how those architectures interact at the household scale — the cumulative effective marginal tax rate at work-incentive earnings points, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#obbba">OBBBA</a>'s procedural-loss vectors, the categorical-eligibility cliffs, and the <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#compass">COMPASS</a> portal as both structural strength and concentrated administrative risk.</p>

<div class="subdomain-list">
<a class="subdomain-row" href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/social-welfare/sub-domains/income-maintenance/">
<span class="subdomain-row__num">1</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__body">
<span class="subdomain-row__title">Income Maintenance</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__desc">The cash-and-credit architecture that catches PA-3 households when the labor market alone doesn't. <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ssi">SSI</a>'s $994 federal payment standard for 2026 against unindexed resource limits of $2,000 / $3,000 since 1989. <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ssdi">SSDI</a> averaging $1,630/month in Pennsylvania; OASDI at $2,071/month average. PA's Group 2 <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#tanf">TANF</a> standard at $403/month for a family of three, against a federal block grant nominally fixed since 1996. Federal <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#eitc">EITC</a> and the new <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#wptc">Working Pennsylvanians Tax Credit</a> at 10% of federal, both conditioned on tax filing. An 18-to-24-month disability-determination pipeline between application and ALJ award where favorable.</span>
</span>
</a>
<a class="subdomain-row" href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/social-welfare/sub-domains/medicaid-coverage/">
<span class="subdomain-row__num">2</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__body">
<span class="subdomain-row__title">Medicaid &amp; Health Coverage Eligibility</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__desc">Approximately 2.99 million Pennsylvanians enrolled in Medicaid in mid-2025 — about 1.1 million in the Group VIII expansion and 2.2 million in HealthChoices managed care — with <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#chip">CHIP</a> extending children's coverage to 319% <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#fpl">FPL</a> and Philadelphia operating the structurally distinctive <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#cbh">Community Behavioral Health</a> entity. The <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#obbba">One Big Beautiful Bill Act</a> (P.L. 119-21, signed July 4, 2025) phases in significant procedural and substantive constraints between 2026 and 2028: 6-month redetermination, 80-hour monthly work reporting, cost-sharing up to $35 per service effective October 2028, restricted qualified-immigrant categories, and a provider-tax safe-harbor stepdown over FY 2028–FY 2034. CBO projects approximately $1 trillion federal spending decrease and 11.8 million coverage losses over FY 2025–FY 2034.</span>
</span>
</a>
<a class="subdomain-row" href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/social-welfare/sub-domains/nutrition-assistance/">
<span class="subdomain-row__num">3</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__body">
<span class="subdomain-row__title">Nutrition Assistance</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__desc"><a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#snap">SNAP</a> under Pennsylvania's <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#bbce">Broad-Based Categorical Eligibility</a> (gross-income threshold raised to 200% FPL; no asset limit) reaches a substantial share of low-income PA-3 households at FY 2026 maximum allotments from $298/month for a single individual to $994/month for a household of four. <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#wic">WIC</a> reaches more than 200,000 Pennsylvanians statewide; the School District operates <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#cep">Community Eligibility Provision</a> at the great majority of its schools, providing free meals to all students without household application. OBBBA reshapes the architecture — <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#abawd">ABAWD</a> age raised from 54 to 64, parent-exemption tightened to youngest child under 14, exemption removals for veterans, homeless, and former foster youth, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#sua">SUA</a> restricted, state administrative cost share rising from 50% to 75% in FY 2027 — with CBO projecting $186 billion in cuts and 2.4 million coverage losses over FY 2025–FY 2034.</span>
</span>
</a>
<a class="subdomain-row" href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/social-welfare/sub-domains/housing-assistance/">
<span class="subdomain-row__num">4</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__body">
<span class="subdomain-row__title">Housing Assistance</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__desc">The <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pha">Philadelphia Housing Authority</a> administers approximately 19,500 <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#hcv">Housing Choice Voucher</a> households and approximately 37,350 total assisted units across HCV, project-based, and public housing combined. The 2010 HCV waitlist of approximately 55,000 took 13 years to clear; the 2023 lottery cap of 10,000 was selected from approximately 36,000-plus applications in a two-week window — a structural supply-vs-demand mismatch of roughly 3-to-1. Voucher issuance does not produce housing receipt: lease-up is documented-failure-prone under landlord-acceptance, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#hqs">housing-quality</a>, and <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#fmr">Fair Market Rent</a> constraints. Pennsylvania has source-of-income protection for voucher-holders only in Philadelphia (and a few other municipalities), not statewide. The roughly 850-plus <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ehv">Emergency Housing Vouchers</a> from the American Rescue Plan are a one-time allocation that closes once turned over.</span>
</span>
</a>
<a class="subdomain-row" href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/social-welfare/sub-domains/disability-support/">
<span class="subdomain-row__num">5</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__body">
<span class="subdomain-row__title">Disability Support</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__desc">SSA's five-step sequential evaluation, administered through the PA Bureau of Disability Determination, operates as the gate to cash benefits, Medicaid for SSI-related categories, Medicare after a statutory 24-month waiting period, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#odp">ODP</a> <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#hcbs">HCBS</a> waivers, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ovr">OVR</a> Financial-Needs-Test exemption, and Ticket-to-Work participation. About two-thirds of initial applications are denied; ALJ hearings can wait 12 months or longer; the cumulative pipeline runs 18–24 months. PA OVR began an Order of Selection waitlist on April 1, 2025, signaling federal VR funding below the level supporting immediate service to all eligible applicants. Approximately 35,000-plus Pennsylvanians receive ODP HCBS waiver services across the four IDD waivers. The <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#able">ABLE</a> age-of-onset threshold rose from 26 to 46 effective January 1, 2026.</span>
</span>
</a>
<a class="subdomain-row" href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/social-welfare/sub-domains/child-family-support/">
<span class="subdomain-row__num">6</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__body">
<span class="subdomain-row__title">Child &amp; Family Support</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__desc">Philadelphia DHS operates ChildLine — approximately 35,000 calls annually — conducts <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#cpsl">CPSL</a> investigations, and manages the <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ioc">Improving Outcomes for Children</a> framework adopted in 2012 with seven contracting organizations operating ten <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#cua">Community Umbrella Agencies</a> across geographic regions. FY 2026 Q1 indicators: 597 families in DHS Prevention Programs; 45% of youth in dependent placement in kinship care (above the 30% national average); 168 youth in dependent residential placement on September 30, 2025. Contractor instability — Turning Points's $40 million operation closed in 2022; Tabor Community Services declined renewal early 2024 — and CUA staff turnover up to 40% annually at some agencies produce service-continuity vulnerability the IOC Scorecard's seven-of-ten "unsatisfactory" baseline reflects.</span>
</span>
</a>
<a class="subdomain-row" href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/social-welfare/sub-domains/elder-support/">
<span class="subdomain-row__num">7</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__body">
<span class="subdomain-row__title">Elder Support</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__desc">Pennsylvania has the third-highest percentage of elderly residents nationally — approximately 2.4 million PA residents 60 and over, approximately 240,000 over age 85 — with one in five older Pennsylvanians living in or near poverty. The architecture combines <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#oaa">OAA</a> Title III supportive services (<a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#acl">ACL</a> → PA Department of Aging → <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pca">Philadelphia Corporation for Aging</a>), Medicaid <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ltss">LTSS</a> via Community HealthChoices, LIFE / PACE integrated comprehensive care, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#liheap">LIHEAP</a> with elder-priority eligibility, and the PA Property Tax / Rent Rebate program (Act 7 of 2023 expanded). Pennsylvania's Lottery-funded elder-program apparatus is a state-distinctive funding architecture providing partial insulation from general-state-budget contestation while linking elder-support funding to PA Lottery sales performance.</span>
</span>
</a>
<a class="subdomain-row subdomain-row--synthesis" href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/social-welfare/sub-domains/cumulative-architecture/">
<span class="subdomain-row__num">8</span>
<span class="subdomain-row__body">
<span class="subdomain-row__title">Cumulative Architecture <span class="subdomain-row__tag">Synthesis sub-domain</span></span>
<span class="subdomain-row__desc">The cross-cutting sub-domain — how the seven program areas above interact at the household scale. The cumulative effective marginal tax rate at the work-incentive interface can approach or exceed 100% at certain earnings points where SNAP, Medicaid expansion, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ccdbg">CCDF</a>, PHA <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#hcv">HCV</a>, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#eitc">EITC</a>, and <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#wptc">WPTC</a> phase-outs and phase-ins overlap. <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#obbba">OBBBA</a>'s 6-month redetermination, work-reporting, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#abawd">ABAWD</a> age extension, and qualified-immigrant restriction operate cumulatively as procedural-loss vectors concentrated in PA-3's heaviest-enrollment sub-areas — CBO's October 28, 2025 supplemental cost estimate directly confirms that 70% of §71107 coverage loss is procedural rather than substantive-eligibility-driven. The SSI resource cliff, the <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#tanf">TANF</a> income cliff at $404 monthly for a family of three, the CCDF exit threshold at 235% FPL, and the HCV income recertification operate as multiple categorical eligibility cliffs at once. The <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#compass">COMPASS</a> unified portal is both structural strength (lowered application friction across TANF, SNAP, Medicaid, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#chip">CHIP</a>, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#liheap">LIHEAP</a> simultaneously) and concentrated administrative risk (multi-program outages affect the same applicant at once). The recurring finding across all eight: statutorily-stable benefits face administratively-and-fiscally-variable receipt.</span>
</span>
</a>
