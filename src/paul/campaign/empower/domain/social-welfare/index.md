# Public Benefits & Social Support

<p class="domain-lead">Social welfare is the domain of public support for people whose circumstances — disability, age, unemployment, family responsibilities, housing instability — put them outside what the labor market alone provides. SNAP, TANF, SSI, SSDI, housing assistance (Section 8, public housing), Medicaid (where it functions as social insurance rather than as health policy proper), unemployment insurance, the EITC, child tax credits. The PA-3 analysis will look at the layered federal/state/county/municipal architecture and at how it actually operates for residents who try to use it.</p>
