# The Gaps — Public Benefits & Safety Net

<p class="domain-lead">A "gap" in this analysis is the distance between the formal legal architecture of a benefit program and the actual receipt it produces for a PA-3 household. D12's gap synthesis is queued. The recurring patterns the verified file documents — statutorily-stable benefits with administratively-and-fiscally-variable receipt; the cumulative effective marginal tax rate at the work-incentive interface where program phase-outs interact; decades of unindexed nominal thresholds (SSI's $2,000 / $3,000 resource limits since 1989; the 1996-frozen TANF block grant); the COMPASS portal's concentration of administrative risk across multiple programs; the OBBBA procedural reshape narrowing the federal floor — will be synthesized here once the per-SD content is drafted.</p>
