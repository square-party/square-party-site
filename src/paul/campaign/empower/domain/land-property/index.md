# Land & Property

<p class="domain-lead">Land and property covers the rules about real property — how it's bought, sold, taxed, zoned, regulated, taken (via eminent domain or other mechanisms), and protected. Housing law lives here, including the architecture of rent regulation, fair housing law, and the public-housing apparatus. Zoning at the municipal level is a particular focus for PA-3, where Philadelphia's zoning code and the surrounding municipalities' codes produce very different patterns of land use.</p>
