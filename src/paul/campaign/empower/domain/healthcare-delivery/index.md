# Healthcare Delivery

<p class="domain-lead">Healthcare Delivery is the provider-side institutional architecture and the federal/state/local regulatory regimes governing it: hospitals (CMS Conditions of Participation, state DOH licensure, Joint Commission); FQHCs (HRSA Section 330); Medicare delivery-payment architecture (Parts A/B/C/D and conditions of payment); 340B; ACA marketplaces and individual insurance market regulation; provider licensure (state medical, nursing, allied-health boards); EMTALA; HIPAA; telehealth regulation; long-term care regulation; healthcare workforce policy. The "healthcare delivery" framing is deliberate against the folk-concept of "healthcare," which sweeps in radically different regulatory architectures under one word.</p>
