# Environment & Natural Resources

<p class="domain-lead">The environment-and-natural-resources domain holds the regulatory architecture of public stewardship over commons that the market won't protect on its own — Clean Air Act, Clean Water Act, ESA, NEPA at the federal layer; the Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection at the state layer; municipal environmental authorities at the local layer. Climate policy lives partly here and partly in Energy; the boundary between the two domains will need to be drawn cleanly when the analysis is done.</p>
