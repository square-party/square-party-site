# Food & Medicine

<p class="domain-lead">Food and medicine are combined here because they share a regulator (the FDA) and a regulatory logic (substances that enter the body, with public-health stakes high enough to justify pre-market review). The domain covers the full chain from production through approval through marketing through post-market surveillance. The boundary with public health is fuzzy and the analysis will need to draw it deliberately.</p>
