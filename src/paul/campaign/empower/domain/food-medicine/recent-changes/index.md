# Recent Changes — Food, Drug &amp; Device

<p class="domain-lead">Eight substantive Material Changes plus supplementary historical context have reshaped the Food, Drug &amp; Device architecture across 2025-2026. The verification cycle's aggregate finding — federal statutes themselves not eroding (HIGH stability composite) but federal agency capacity to administer them substantively eroded in 2025-2026 (5 of 6 D4 SDs at HIGH or EXTREME admin vulnerability); the MAHA-era three-pattern co-occurrence (rule withdrawal + capacity erosion + data-infrastructure rollback documented as co-occurring through separate decisions, not inferred as a coordinated program); the XC-11 cross-cutting independent / small organization differential across all sub-domains; the Compound Disadvantage Geography Matrix as central D4 finding-presentation infrastructure — is composed of these events. <strong>The most consequential federal-administrative change is [MC-07](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D4-food-drug/D4_foodMed_verified_2026-04-29.md#mc-07) <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#fda">FDA</a> RIF detailed quantification</strong> — April 1, 2025 RIF affected ~3,500 personnel = 19% of workforce; <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#cder">CDER</a> lost ~1,000 employees over three months; <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#cdrh">CDRH</a> ~260 fired including 40 from Digital Health Center of Excellence; CTP Director Brian King removed; office-level decimation across CDER Office of Medical Policy + <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#cber">CBER</a> Office of Regulatory Operations + CDRH Office of Women's Health; Rhode Island injunction reach. <strong>The most consequential data-infrastructure change is [MC-08](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D4-food-drug/D4_foodMed_verified_2026-04-29.md#mc-08) <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ers"><a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#usda">USDA</a> ERS</a> termination</strong> — announced September 20, 2025; final ERR-358 December 2025 post-43-day government shutdown; 13.7% household FI 2024 (Black 24.4% / Latinx 20.2% / White 10.1%); 47.4M people affected; ERS budget $310.5M = 0.2% USDA budget. <strong>The most consequential substantive program changes are [MC-04](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D4-food-drug/D4_foodMed_verified_2026-04-29.md#mc-04) <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ira">IRA</a> Round 2 MFPs + <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#obbba">OBBBA</a> orphan-drug broadening + <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#mfn">GENEROUS Model</a> launch</strong> (Round 2 effective January 1, 2027; 38-84% off; $12B annual Part D + $685M OOP savings) and <strong>[MC-05](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D4-food-drug/D4_foodMed_verified_2026-04-29.md#mc-05) Casgevy + Lyfgenia uptake quantified</strong> (~164 cumulative US uptake through 2025 substantially confirms low-uptake framing of the Philadelphia paradox). <strong>The most consequential rule-availability changes are [MC-06](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D4-food-drug/D4_foodMed_verified_2026-04-29.md#mc-06) menthol withdrawal date precision</strong> (January 21, 2025; RIN 0910-AI60; <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#aatclc">AATCLC</a> v. <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#hhs">HHS</a> pending in N.D. Cal.) and <strong>[MC-03](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D4-food-drug/D4_foodMed_verified_2026-04-29.md#mc-03) <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#fsis">FSIS</a> <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#nrte">NRTE</a> breaded stuffed chicken indefinite delay</strong> (December 2025; cheaper-cut Salmonella exposure pathway quantitatively confirmed at 24.2% comminuted chicken). <strong>The most consequential federal architecture expansion is [MC-01](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D4-food-drug/D4_foodMed_verified_2026-04-29.md#mc-01) <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#dea">DEA</a> Marijuana Schedule III</strong> (effective April 28, 2026; Trump EO 14370; only D4 sub-domain documenting expansion rather than withdrawal). <strong>The most consequential federal-state operational change is [MC-02](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D4-food-drug/D4_foodMed_verified_2026-04-29.md#mc-02) <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#acip">ACIP</a> charter rewrite post-court-block</strong> (March 16, 2026 Judge Brian E. <p>The federal-food-drug-device-controlled-substances statutory foundation: Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act of 1938 (FFDCA; 21 U.S.C. § 301 et seq.) — Chapter IV foods (21 U.S.C. §§ 341-350l), Chapter V drugs and devices (21 U.S.C. §§ 351-360fff), Chapter IX tobacco (21 U.S.C. § 387 et seq.; added by 2009 <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#tca">TCA</a>). Egg Products Inspection Act (<a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#epia">EPIA</a>; 21 U.S.C. § 1031 et seq.). Controlled Substances Act of 1970 (CSA; 21 U.S.C. § 801 et seq.) — Schedule I-V framework; DEA registration; quotas; suspicious-order monitoring. <strong>Statutory layer composite: HIGH stability — federal statutes themselves are not eroding; the foundational architecture remains; agency-layer and rule-availability layers are where 2025-2026 erosion is documented.</strong></p>
<p class="changelog-entry__refs">Affects: all seven sub-domains. Sources: FFDCA at <a href="https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/21">21 U.S.C.</a>; FMIA + PPIA + EPIA at 21 U.S.C. §§ 601 / 451 / 1031; CSA at 21 U.S.C. § 801 et seq.</p>

<h2 class="changelog-entry__title">Family Smoking Prevention and Tobacco Control Act of 2009 — FDA tobacco regulatory authority</h2>
</header>
<p>The Family Smoking Prevention and Tobacco Control Act of 2009 (FSPTCA / TCA; Pub. L. 111-31; 21 U.S.C. § 387 et seq.) established FDA tobacco regulatory authority — <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pmta">PMTA</a> framework; modified-risk tobacco; flavor restrictions; minimum age federalized as Tobacco 21 in 2019. Bipartisan 2009 enactment; statutory stability HIGH. The TCA grounds the FDA Center for Tobacco Products (CTP) at Silver Spring, MD with field activities through FDA <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#oii">OII</a> Philadelphia District. The menthol cigarette ban proposed rulemaking originated in 2022 under TCA authority; the January 21, 2025 withdrawal operates within the TCA framework without altering the statutory authority itself.</p>
<p class="changelog-entry__refs">Affects: <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/food-medicine/sub-domains/tobacco/">Tobacco</a> (principal anchor at SD5). Sources: FSPTCA at <a href="https://www.congress.gov/bill/111th-congress/house-bill/1256">Pub. L. 111-31</a>; 21 U.S.C. § 387 et seq.</p>
