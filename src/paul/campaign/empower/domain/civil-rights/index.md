# Civil Rights

<p class="domain-lead">Civil Rights is the cross-cutting anti-discrimination enforcement architecture: Titles VI, VII, IX of the Civil Rights Act; ADA; FHA; Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act; Voting Rights Act enforcement provisions; enforcement bodies (DOJ Civil Rights Division, EEOC, HUD FHEO, ED OCR, HHS OCR). Domain-specific anti-discrimination provisions live in their substantive home (Title VII employment in Labor & Employment; Title IX education in Education; FHA in Land & Property). This domain owns the cross-cutting enforcement architecture and the structural questions it raises.</p>
