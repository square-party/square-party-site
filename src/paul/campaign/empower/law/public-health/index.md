# Legal text appendix — Public Health

<p class="domain-lead">The legal chain that produces PA-3's public-health architecture, organized constitutional → federal → state → local. The constitutional foundation runs through the U.S. Spending Clause for federal program authority, the 10th Amendment for state police-power grounding, and Article I § 27 of the Pennsylvania Constitution (the Environmental Rights Amendment, grounded in <em>PEDF v. Commonwealth</em>). The federal substantive layer is statutorily stable but operates under court-imposed checks on the most aggressive elements of MAHA-era institutional restructuring (Braidwood preserving the ACA § 2713 preventive-services mandate; Murphy restoring the pre-January 2026 federal childhood vaccine schedule); the state layer operates the substantive PA programs without divergent posture; the Philadelphia layer delivers the front-end administration. For the analytical treatment of how each instrument operates and where its gaps fall, see the seven <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/">D2 sub-domain pages</a>.</p>

## Constitutional foundation

### U.S. Constitution

**Article I, § 8 — Spending Clause** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/constitution/articlei#section8)).<br>
Authorizes federal financial assistance to state and local public-health programs subject to federal program requirements. The constitutional basis for PHEP, § 317 immunization program, Title V MCH Block Grant, § 330 FQHC program, Head Start, CCDBG, WIOA Title II, and the entirety of D2's federal-floor architecture. Conditional-spending principles articulated in *South Dakota v. Dole*, 483 U.S. 203 (1987) govern state-plan submission requirements.<br>
*Cited in:* every D2 sub-domain.

**10th Amendment** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/constitution/tenth_amendment)).<br>
Reserves the underlying police power for public-health regulation with the states. Federal authority operates through conditional spending and through interstate-commerce-grounded statutes; state public-health authority (PA Disease Prevention and Control Law of 1955) rests on the 10th Amendment foundation.<br>
*Cited in:* every D2 sub-domain.

**14th Amendment, § 1 — Equal Protection / Due Process** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/constitution/amendmentxiv)).<br>
Grounds the civil-rights overlays on federally-funded public-health programs (Title VI; Title IX; Section 504).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/access-fqhc/">Health Access</a>.

### Key foundational cases

***Kennedy v. Braidwood Management, Inc.*, 606 U.S. 748 (June 27, 2025)**.<br>
Decided 6-3 (Kavanaugh majority joined by Roberts, Sotomayor, Kagan, Barrett, Jackson; Thomas dissent joined by Alito, Gorsuch). SCOTUS reversed the Fifth Circuit and upheld the ACA § 2713 preventive services mandate. USPSTF members are *inferior officers* properly appointed under the Appointments Clause because the Secretary of HHS may remove them at will and review or block recommendations before they take effect. Cancer screenings, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#prep">PrEP</a>, tobacco cessation, and other <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#uspstf">USPSTF</a> A/B services remain mandate-protected nationwide. Braidwood did not directly address <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#acip">ACIP</a> (vaccines) or <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#hrsa">HRSA</a> Women's Preventive Services.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/communicable-disease/">Communicable Disease Control</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/maternal-child/">Maternal &amp; Child Health</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/chronic-disease/">Chronic Disease</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/access-fqhc/">Health Access</a>.

***American Academy of Pediatrics et al. v. Kennedy et al.***, U.S. District Court for the District of Massachusetts (1st Circuit), Judge Brian E. Murphy.<br>
Preliminary injunction issued March 16, 2026. Stays the January 5, 2026 CDC Decision Memo demoting seven vaccines, blocks the reconstituted ACIP membership (only 6 of 15 had meaningful vaccine expertise per the court's <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#faca">FACA</a> analysis), invalidates three reconstituted-ACIP votes including the December 2025 hep B birth-dose downgrade, and overturns the May 2025 Secretarial Directive halting COVID-19 vaccine recommendations for pregnant women and healthy children. The pre-January 2026 federal childhood schedule is effectively restored. HHS appealed to the First Circuit.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/infrastructure/">Public Health Infrastructure</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/communicable-disease/">Communicable Disease Control</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/maternal-child/">Maternal &amp; Child Health</a>.

***State of Rhode Island v. Trump***, Case No. 1:25-cv-00128 (D.R.I.), Judge Melissa DuBose; on appeal as No. 26-1070 to the First Circuit.<br>
Originally entered July 2025; narrowed August 12, 2025 to cover FDA's Center for Tobacco Products, most CDC offices, HHS Office of Head Start, and HHS <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#aspe">Office of the Assistant Secretary for Planning and Evaluation</a>. The narrowing specifically excluded CDC's Immunization Services Division and Partnership and Vaccine Equity Branch, which were terminated.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/infrastructure/">Public Health Infrastructure</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/communicable-disease/">Communicable Disease Control</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/chronic-disease/">Chronic Disease</a>.

***United States v. Safehouse***, 985 F.3d 225 (3d Cir. 2021).<br>
Held that a supervised-consumption site would violate CSA § 856. SCOTUS denied cert October 2021. April 4, 2024 Judge McHugh dismissed Safehouse's RFRA counterclaim; June 2024 Safehouse Notice of Appeal to 3d Cir. (RFRA dimension); 3d Cir. status pending.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/substance-use/">Substance Use &amp; Harm Reduction</a>.

***Pennsylvania Environmental Defense Foundation v. Commonwealth***, 161 A.3d 911 (Pa. 2017).<br>
Held that the Commonwealth holds public natural resources as a trustee under PA Const. Art. I § 27, with corresponding fiduciary duties to current and future generations. Provides a state-level structural environmental rights protection independent of the federal framework.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/environmental-health/">Environmental Health</a>.

### Pennsylvania Constitution

**Article I § 27 — Environmental Rights Amendment** (ratified 1971).<br>
"The people have a right to clean air, pure water, and to the preservation of the natural, scenic, historic and esthetic values of the environment. Pennsylvania's public natural resources are the common property of all the people, including generations yet to come. As trustee of these resources, the Commonwealth shall conserve and maintain them for the benefit of all the people." Interpreted in *PEDF v. Commonwealth* (2017).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/public-health/sub-domains/environmental-health/">Environmental Health</a>.
