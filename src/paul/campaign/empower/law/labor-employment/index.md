# Legal text appendix — Labor &amp; Employment

<p class="domain-lead">The legal chain that produces PA-3's Labor &amp; Employment architecture, organized constitutional → federal → state → local. The federal substantive layer threads through <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#flsa">FLSA</a>, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#fmla">FMLA</a>, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#osh-act-osha">OSH Act</a>, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#nlra">NLRA</a>, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#title-vii">Title VII</a>, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#adea">ADEA</a>, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ada-title-i">ADA Title I</a>, EPA, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pwfa">PWFA</a>, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pump-act">PUMP Act</a>, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#futa">FUTA</a> / SSA Title III and IX, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#wioa">WIOA</a>, Registered Apprenticeship, <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#second-chance-act">Second Chance Act</a>, and <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#section-1981">42 U.S.C. § 1981</a>; the regulatory architecture above the statutory floor is currently operating under substantial disruption — MC01 EO 14173 (January 21, 2025) revoking EO 11246 and eliminating <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ofccp">OFCCP</a> at the EO 11246 level; MC02 <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#nlrb">NLRB</a> GC Crystal Carey confirmed December 2025; MC03 February 2026 NLRB final rule formally withdrawing the Biden 2023 joint-employer rule and reinstating the narrow direct-and-immediate-control standard; MC04 OSHA heat illness rulemaking stalled. The state layer threads through PA Constitution Art. III § 25; PA Minimum Wage Act at 43 P.S. § 333.101 et seq. with § 333.115 (Act 1 of 2006) preempting local minimum-wage authority; PA Wage Payment and Collection Law at 43 P.S. § 260.1 et seq.; PA Workers' Compensation Act at 77 P.S. § 1 et seq.; PA OSHA state plan covering public-sector employees only; PA Labor Relations Act at 43 P.S. § 211.1 et seq.; Public Employee Relations Act (Act 195 of 1970) at 43 P.S. § 1101.101 et seq.; PA Human Relations Act at 43 P.S. § 951 et seq.; PA UC Law at 43 P.S. § 751 et seq.; MC05 PA Bulletin official notice effective January 1, 2026 freezing maximum UC weekly benefit at $605/week under solvency trigger; MC06 PA UC Trust Fund confirmed below solvency. The Philadelphia layer threads through Philadelphia Code Title 9 employment ordinances — Wage Theft Ordinance (Ch. 9-3100); Promoting Healthy Families and Workplaces Ordinance (Ch. 9-4100 sick leave); Fair Workweek Employment Standards Ordinance (Ch. 9-4600); Domestic Workers Bill of Rights (Ch. 9-4500, 2019); Philadelphia Fair Practices Ordinance (Ch. 9-1100); Philadelphia Fair Criminal Record Screening Standards Ordinance / Ban the Box (Ch. 9-3500). For the analytical treatment of how each instrument operates and where its gaps fall, see the seven <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/">D10 sub-domain pages</a>.</p>

## Constitutional foundation

### U.S. Constitution

**Article I § 8 — Commerce Clause and Spending Clause** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/constitution/articlei#section8)).<br>
Commerce Clause supplies federal authority for FLSA, NLRA, OSHA, and anti-discrimination statutes — each premised on the regulation of employment as commerce. Spending Clause is the basis for conditional federal grants to states for workforce development programs (WIOA), unemployment compensation systems, and vocational rehabilitation.<br>
*Cited in:* every D10 sub-domain.

**13th and 14th Amendments** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/constitution/amendmentxiii); [14th](https://www.law.cornell.edu/constitution/amendmentxiv)).<br>
Ground the anti-discrimination employment statutes (Title VII; ADEA; ADA Title I; PWFA; [GINA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#gina)) as expressions of Congress's power to enforce substantive equality norms.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/anti-discrimination/">Anti-Discrimination in Employment</a>.

### Key foundational cases

***Bostock v. Clayton County***, **590 U.S. 644 (2020)** ([Justia](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/590/644/)).<br>
Title VII's prohibition of "sex" discrimination encompasses discrimination on the basis of sexual orientation and gender identity.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/anti-discrimination/">Anti-Discrimination in Employment</a>.

***McDonnell Douglas Corp. v. Green***, **411 U.S. 792 (1973)** ([Justia](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/411/792/)).<br>
Three-stage burden-shifting framework for disparate-treatment discrimination claims: plaintiff prima facie case; employer legitimate non-discriminatory reason; plaintiff pretext showing.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/anti-discrimination/">Anti-Discrimination in Employment</a>.

***Griggs v. Duke Power Co.***, **401 U.S. 424 (1971)** ([Justia](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/401/424/)).<br>
Established disparate-impact theory under Title VII for facially neutral employment practices producing protected-class disparate effects.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/anti-discrimination/">Anti-Discrimination in Employment</a>.

***Columbia University***, **364 NLRB No. 90 (2016)**.<br>
Held that graduate student employees at private universities are statutory employees under the NLRA entitled to collective bargaining rights. Challenge filed February 10, 2026 by a Cornell doctoral student with NLRB GC Crystal Carey requesting reconsideration; GC Carey is management-side and expected to be receptive to overturning 2016 coverage.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/labor-relations/">Labor Relations and Collective Bargaining</a>.

***Chamber of Commerce of the United States v. NLRB*** (E.D. Tex. March 2024).<br>
Preliminary injunction vacating the Biden 2023 joint-employer rule. The vacatur was followed in February 2026 by Trump-era NLRB final rule formally withdrawing the Biden 2023 rule and reinstating the narrow "direct and immediate control" standard (MC03).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/labor-relations/">Labor Relations and Collective Bargaining</a>.

***State of Texas v. DOL*** (E.D. Tex. November 15, 2024).<br>
Nationwide vacatur of the 2024 Biden DOL final rule raising the FLSA white-collar overtime exemption salary threshold to $58,656/year. Threshold reverts to $35,568/year under the 2019 rule. Standard 17 applied at SD2: both baselines preserved.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/employment-standards/">Employment Standards</a>.

***Camara v. Municipal Court***, **387 U.S. 523 (1967)** ([Justia](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/387/523/)).<br>
Administrative-search doctrine governing OSHA administrative inspection authority (cross-reference D7 SD7 for L&amp;I housing-code-enforcement application).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/workplace-safety/">Workplace Safety and Workers' Compensation</a>.

***Navickas v. UCBR***. PA willful-misconduct standard governing UC eligibility under 43 P.S. § 802(e).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/unemployment-compensation/">Unemployment Compensation</a>.

***Pugh v. Holmes***, **486 Pa. 272 (1979)**. Implied warranty of habitability under PA landlord-tenant law (cross-reference D7 SD3 / D7 SD7 for housing-quality enforcement application via tenant rent-withholding).

### Pennsylvania Constitution

**PA Constitution Article III § 25** (PA Const. art. III § 25).<br>
"The General Assembly may enact laws requiring the payment by employers, or employers and employees jointly, of reasonable compensation for injuries to employees arising in the course of their employment." Textually explicit constitutional grant of legislative authority over labor regulation. Constitutional foundation for the PA Minimum Wage Act, the Wage Payment and Collection Law, the [PLRA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#plra), [PERA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pera), the [PHRA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#phra), and the Pennsylvania Workers' Compensation Act.<br>
*Cited in:* every D10 sub-domain.

## Federal statutes — wage and hour

**Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)** ([29 U.S.C. § 201 et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/chapter-8)).<br>
Enacted 1938; extensively amended. Minimum wage at 29 U.S.C. § 206(a)(1) — $7.25/hour unchanged since July 24, 2009. Tipped employees: cash minimum $2.13/hour at § 203(m)(2)(A). Overtime at 29 U.S.C. § 207(a)(1) — 1.5× the regular rate beyond 40 hours per workweek. White-collar exemption — threshold reverted to $35,568/year ($684/week) under 2019 rule following <em>State of Texas v. DOL</em> November 15, 2024 vacatur.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/employment-standards/">Employment Standards</a>; cross-references SD1, SD3, SD6 for FLSA-side economic-reality misclassification cross-test.

**Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA)** ([29 U.S.C. § 2601 et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/chapter-28)).<br>
50-employee employer threshold within 75 miles; employee employed 12 months; employee worked 1,250 hours in prior year. Entitlement: 12 workweeks unpaid, job-protected leave per year.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/employment-standards/">Employment Standards</a>.

**PUMP Act for Nursing Mothers** ([29 U.S.C. § 218d](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/218d)).<br>
Effective December 29, 2022. Requires reasonable break time and private space for pumping for up to one year after birth; extended coverage to salaried workers previously excluded.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/employment-standards/">Employment Standards</a>.

## Federal statutes — workplace safety

**Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSH Act)** ([29 U.S.C. § 651 et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/chapter-15)).<br>
Enacted December 29, 1970. General Duty Clause at 29 U.S.C. § 654(a)(1): employers must furnish employment "free from recognized hazards that are causing or are likely to cause death or serious physical harm." Specific standards: Construction lead at 29 C.F.R. § 1926.62; Bloodborne Pathogens at 29 C.F.R. § 1910.1030; Hazard Communication at 29 C.F.R. § 1910.1200; Construction asbestos at 29 C.F.R. § 1926.1101. Whistleblower protection at 29 U.S.C. § 660(c) — 30-day filing deadline among the shortest of any federal whistleblower statute. **MC04 OSHA heat illness rulemaking stalled**: NPRM published August 30, 2024 (89 Fed. Reg. 70706); post-hearing comment period closed October 30, 2025; no final rule target date; OSHA NEP expiring April 8, 2026.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/workplace-safety/">Workplace Safety and Workers' Compensation</a>.

## Federal statutes — labor relations

**National Labor Relations Act (NLRA)** ([29 U.S.C. § 151 et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/chapter-7)).<br>
Enacted 1935; amended by Taft-Hartley Act (1947), Landrum-Griffin Act (1959), Health Care Amendment (1974). Section 7 rights at 29 U.S.C. § 157 (self-organization; collective bargaining; mutual aid or protection); Section 8(a) Employer Unfair Labor Practices at § 158(a) (interference, discrimination, refusal to bargain); coverage exclusions at § 152(3) (agricultural workers; domestic workers in private homes; supervisors; independent contractors; public employees); remedies at § 160 limited to reinstatement and back pay minus interim earnings — no punitive damages; no compensatory emotional distress damages; no attorney's fees against the employer. <strong>MC03 — February 2026 NLRB final rule</strong> formally withdrawing the Biden 2023 joint-employer rule and reinstating the traditional narrow "direct and immediate control" standard. <strong>MC02 — NLRB General Counsel Crystal Carey</strong> confirmed by Senate December 2025; James R. Murphy and Scott Mayer confirmed as Board Members.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/labor-relations/">Labor Relations and Collective Bargaining</a>.

## Federal statutes — anti-discrimination

**Title VII of the Civil Rights Act** ([42 U.S.C. § 2000e et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/chapter-21/subchapter-VI)).<br>
Coverage: employers with 15+ employees. Protected bases: race, color, religion, sex (per <em>Bostock</em> includes sexual orientation and gender identity), national origin. 300-day [EEOC](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#eeoc) filing deadline in Pennsylvania. Damages cap: $300,000 maximum for 500+ employee employers; sliding scale.

**42 U.S.C. § 1981** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/1981)).<br>
**No damages cap.** Available in parallel with Title VII for race discrimination claims.

**Age Discrimination in Employment Act (ADEA)** ([29 U.S.C. § 621 et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/chapter-14)).<br>
20+ employee employers; workers 40+. SOL: 300 days.

**Americans with Disabilities Act Title I** ([42 U.S.C. § 12111 et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/chapter-126/subchapter-I)).<br>
15+ employee employers. ADAAA of 2008 broadly expanded "disability" definition.

**Pregnant Workers Fairness Act (PWFA)** ([42 U.S.C. § 2000gg et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/chapter-21/subchapter-VIII)).<br>
Effective June 27, 2023; EEOC final rule effective June 18, 2024.

**Executive Order 11246 — REVOKED by Executive Order 14173 (January 21, 2025).** EO 14173 "Ending Illegal Discrimination and Restoring Merit-Based Opportunity," signed January 21, 2025, revoked EO 11246 entirely. Secretary's Order 03-2025 (January 24, 2025) ordered OFCCP to cease all EO 11246 investigative and enforcement activity. OFCCP reduced from approximately 479 staff across 55 offices to approximately 50 employees in 4 regional locations. Section 503 (disability) and VEVRAA (veterans) enforcement resumed July 2, 2025 under Secretary's Order 08-2025. FY2026 OBBBA (P.L. 119-21, signed July 4, 2025) eliminates OFCCP funding entirely pending Congressional action.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/anti-discrimination/">Anti-Discrimination in Employment</a>; <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/labor-market/">Labor Market Infrastructure and Governance</a>.

## Federal statutes — credit and unemployment

**Federal Unemployment Tax Act (FUTA)** ([26 U.S.C. § 3301 et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/subtitle-C/chapter-23)).<br>
6.0% federal payroll tax on employers on first $7,000 of each employee's wages per year; effectively reduced to 0.6% after credit for approved state programs.

**Social Security Act Title III** ([42 U.S.C. § 501 et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/chapter-7/subchapter-III)) and **Title IX** ([42 U.S.C. § 1101 et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/chapter-7/subchapter-IX)).<br>
Title III: grants to states for UC administration. Title IX: Federal Unemployment Trust Fund; federal extended benefits trigger mechanism.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/unemployment-compensation/">Unemployment Compensation</a>.

## Federal statutes — workforce development

**Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA)** ([29 U.S.C. § 3101 et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/chapter-32)).<br>
Enacted July 22, 2014. Title I-B Adult / Dislocated Worker / Youth Programs; Title II Adult Education and Literacy; Title III Wagner-Peyser Act Services; Title IV Vocational Rehabilitation. Local Workforce Development Board provisions at 29 U.S.C. § 3122. Youth out-of-school provision: 75% of youth funding for out-of-school youth per WIOA § 128(b)(4).

**National Apprenticeship Act (Registered Apprenticeship)** ([29 U.S.C. § 50](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/50); [29 C.F.R. Part 29](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-A/part-29)).<br>
DOL Office of Apprenticeship registers programs; sets standards: minimum 2,000 hours on-the-job learning; related technical instruction; progressive wage schedule; written apprenticeship standards.

**Second Chance Act** ([42 U.S.C. § 17501 et seq.](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/chapter-229)).<br>
Enacted 2008. Grants for reentry services including employment services, mentoring, and supportive housing.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/workforce-development/">Workforce Development and Economic Mobility</a>.

## Pennsylvania statutes

**Pennsylvania Minimum Wage Act ([PMWA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pmwa))** ([43 P.S. § 333.101 et seq.](https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/uconsCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&yr=1968&sessInd=0&smthLwInd=0&act=5)).<br>
PA minimum wage: $7.25/hour (matching federal floor; not raised since 2009). PA tipped minimum: $2.83/hour. **§ 333.115 (Act 1 of 2006)** expressly preempts political subdivisions from enacting or enforcing any ordinance, resolution, regulation, or rule concerning minimum wage.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/employment-standards/">Employment Standards</a>.

**Pennsylvania Wage Payment and Collection Law ([WPCL](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#wpcl))** ([43 P.S. § 260.1 et seq.](https://www.legis.state.pa.us)).<br>
Private right of action for failure to pay agreed wages. Liquidated damages up to 25% of amount owed or $500, whichever is greater at 43 P.S. § 260.10. Attorney's fees available. SOL: 3 years.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/employment-standards/">Employment Standards</a>.

**Pennsylvania Workers' Compensation Act ([WCA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#wca))** ([77 P.S. § 1 et seq.](https://www.legis.state.pa.us)).<br>
No-fault insurance system. Independent-contractor exclusion is the key coverage boundary. Panel-of-physicians provision at 77 P.S. § 306(f.1)(1)(i) — if employer posts a proper panel, the injured worker must treat with a panel physician for the first 90 days. TTD benefits at 66⅔% of AWW up to SAWW maximum. **Uninsured Employers Guaranty Fund ([UEGF](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#uegf))** at 77 P.S. § 1701 et seq. Act 111 of 2018 IRE provisions.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/workplace-safety/">Workplace Safety and Workers' Compensation</a>.

**Pennsylvania Labor Relations Act (PLRA)** ([43 P.S. § 211.1 et seq.](https://www.legis.state.pa.us)).<br>
Enacted 1937. Parallels NLRA for private-sector employers below NLRB jurisdictional thresholds. [PLRB](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#plrb) adjudicates.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/labor-relations/">Labor Relations and Collective Bargaining</a>.

**Public Employee Relations Act (PERA), Act 195 of 1970** ([43 P.S. § 1101.101 et seq.](https://www.legis.state.pa.us)).<br>
Governs collective bargaining rights for Commonwealth and local government employees in Pennsylvania. Right to strike generally available.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/labor-relations/">Labor Relations and Collective Bargaining</a>.

**Pennsylvania Human Relations Act (PHRA)** ([43 P.S. § 951 et seq.](https://www.legis.state.pa.us)).<br>
Coverage: employers with **4 or more employees** — significantly lower threshold than Title VII's 15-employee floor. Protected classes include race, color, religious creed, ancestry, age (40-70), sex, national origin, disability, familial status. [PHRC](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#phrc) dual-filing with EEOC. SOL: 180 days.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/anti-discrimination/">Anti-Discrimination in Employment</a>.

**Pennsylvania UC Law** ([43 P.S. § 751 et seq.](https://www.legis.state.pa.us)).<br>
Coverage exclusions at § 753(l) — self-employment; independent contractors; certain agricultural workers; certain domestic workers; certain religious organization employees; certain student workers. Base-year wage requirement at § 801. Separation eligibility at § 802 with willful misconduct at § 402(e); voluntary-quit good-cause at § 402(b); domestic violence good-cause at § 802(e)(3). Able and available at § 801(d)(1). PA UC ABC test at § 753(l) for worker classification. **MC05 PA Bulletin official notice effective January 1, 2026** freezing maximum weekly benefit at $605/week under solvency trigger (Standard 17 prior-method baseline $854/week preserved). **MC06 PA UC Trust Fund confirmed below solvency** (PA L&amp;I actuarial evaluation 2025; PA House co-sponsorship memorandum May 2025).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/unemployment-compensation/">Unemployment Compensation</a>.

**PA Unfair Trade Practices and Consumer Protection Law (UTPCPL)** ([73 P.S. § 201-1 et seq.](https://www.legis.state.pa.us)).<br>
Consumer-protection cause of action used in some tenant cases and predatory-employment-practice cases.

## Philadelphia local statutes

### Employment standards

**Philadelphia Promoting Healthy Families and Workplaces Ordinance (Paid Sick Leave)** ([Phila. Code Ch. 9-4100](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/philadelphia/latest/philadelphia_pa)).<br>
Employers with 10+ employees: 1 hour paid sick leave per 40 hours worked; maximum 40 hours/year.

**Philadelphia Fair Workweek Employment Standards Ordinance** ([Phila. Code Ch. 9-4600](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/philadelphia/latest/philadelphia_pa)).<br>
Covers large retail, food service, and hospitality employers (250+ employees nationally; 30+ locations). 14-day advance notice; right to decline shifts; predictability pay; right to rest; written good-faith estimate of expected hours at hire.

**Philadelphia Wage Theft Ordinance** ([Phila. Code Ch. 9-3100](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/philadelphia/latest/philadelphia_pa)).<br>
Enacted 2016. Supplements PA WPCL with a local enforcement mechanism. [PDOL](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pdol) enforces.

**Philadelphia Domestic Workers Bill of Rights ([DWBOR](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#dwbor))** ([Phila. Code Ch. 9-4500](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/philadelphia/latest/philadelphia_pa)).<br>
Enacted 2019. Covers domestic workers — a category explicitly excluded from NLRA collective bargaining rights at 29 U.S.C. § 152(3). Both/And exemplar for MC29.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/employment-standards/">Employment Standards</a>; <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/labor-relations/">Labor Relations and Collective Bargaining</a>.

### Anti-discrimination

**Philadelphia Fair Practices Ordinance ([PFPO](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pfpo))** ([Phila. Code Ch. 9-1100](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/philadelphia/latest/philadelphia_pa)).<br>
Coverage: employers with **1 or more employees** — broadest anti-discrimination coverage threshold in the PA-3 regulatory architecture. Protected classes include all PHRA classes plus sexual orientation, gender identity, marital status, domestic or sexual violence victim status. Enforcement: [PCHR](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pchr).

**Philadelphia Fair Criminal Record Screening Standards Ordinance (Ban the Box)** ([Phila. Code Ch. 9-3500](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/philadelphia/latest/philadelphia_pa)).<br>
Enacted 2011. Coverage: employers with **10 or more employees** operating in Philadelphia. Requirements: remove criminal history inquiries from applications; post-conditional-offer inquiry only; individualized assessment required; 10 business days for applicant response; written final notice of adverse action. PCHR enforcement.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/anti-discrimination/">Anti-Discrimination in Employment</a>; <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/sub-domains/workforce-development/">Workforce Development and Economic Mobility</a>.

## Executive Orders and Material Changes inventory

### MC01 OFCCP/EO 11246 elimination

**Executive Order 14173 — "Ending Illegal Discrimination and Restoring Merit-Based Opportunity"** (signed January 21, 2025).<br>
Revokes EO 11246 entirely.

**Secretary's Order 03-2025** (January 24, 2025). Orders OFCCP to immediately cease and desist all investigative and enforcement activity under EO 11246; places Section 503 and VEVRAA activity in abeyance pending further guidance.

**Secretary's Order 08-2025** (July 2, 2025). Section 503 (disability) and VEVRAA (veterans) enforcement resumed.

**FY2026 OBBBA (P.L. 119-21)** signed July 4, 2025. Eliminates OFCCP funding entirely pending Congressional action; OFCCP operating under continuing resolution.

### MC04 OSHA heat illness rulemaking

**89 Fed. Reg. 70706** (August 30, 2024 NPRM). Informal public hearing held June 16-July 2, 2025. **Federal Register extension notice September 25, 2025** — post-hearing comment period extended and closed October 30, 2025. No final rule; OSHA NEP on heat-related hazards expiring April 8, 2026.

### MC05 / MC06 PA UC Trust Fund and benefit

**PA Bulletin official notice effective January 1, 2026** — PA UC maximum weekly benefit frozen at $605/week under solvency trigger; 3.2% solvency reduction applies to all benefit payments. Trigger percentage fell below 250% solvency threshold as of July 1, 2025. Standard 17 governmental-score updating applied: $854/week preserved as prior-method baseline.

**PA L&amp;I actuarial evaluation 2025** and **PA House of Representatives co-sponsorship memorandum May 2025** confirm PA UC Trust Fund below solvency; 10+ years to full solvency.

### Supplementary federal litigation references

***Ultima Services Corp. v. USDA*** (E.D. Tenn. 2023). Federal court holding leading to SBA 8(a) program race-neutral restructuring (D8 MC02 carry-forward to D10 SD5; SBA formal guidance January 22, 2026 confirming race-neutral program).
