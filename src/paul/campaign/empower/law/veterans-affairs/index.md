# Legal text appendix — Veterans Affairs

<p class="domain-lead">The legal chain that produces PA-3's Veterans Affairs architecture, organized constitutional → federal → state → local. The federal substantive layer is consolidated under Title 38 of the U.S. Code — Chapter 11 (disability compensation); Chapter 15 (pension including aid-and-attendance enhanced pension); Chapter 17 (hospital care and medical services; <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#vha">VHA</a>); Chapter 31 (Veteran Readiness &amp; Employment); Chapter 33 (<a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#post-9-11-gi-bill">Post-9/11 GI Bill</a>); Chapter 37 (home loan guaranty); Chapter 71 (Board of Veterans' Appeals); Chapter 72 (Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims) — plus the pro-claimant adjudication standards at 38 U.S.C. § 5107 (benefit of the doubt) and § 5103A (duty to assist), the <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#discharge-characterization">discharge characterization</a> eligibility gate at 38 U.S.C. § 5303 (MC45 substrate), the <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#vso">VSO</a> accreditation framework at 38 U.S.C. § 5902 and § 5904, the <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#va">VA</a> Vets First Contracting Program at 38 U.S.C. § 8127, and the <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#vevraa">VEVRAA</a> federal-contractor veteran affirmative-action obligation at 38 U.S.C. § 4212. Major recent statutory expansions thread through the Post-9/11 GI Bill (P.L. 110-252, 2008); the Veterans' Judicial Review Act (P.L. 100-527, 1988) elevating VA to Cabinet-level Department and creating <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#cavc">CAVC</a>; the Appeals Modernization Act (P.L. 115-55, 2017); the <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#forever-gi-bill">Forever GI Bill</a> / Harry W. Colmery Act (P.L. 115-48, 2017); the <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#mission-act">VA MISSION Act</a> (P.L. 115-182, 2018); the FY2021 NDAA § 9103 <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#hud-vash">HUD-VASH</a> <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#oth">OTH</a>-discharge extension (P.L. 116-283); the <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pact-act">PACT Act</a> (P.L. 117-168, 2022); the FY2024 <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ndaa">NDAA</a> <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#sdvosb">SDVOSB</a> 5% goal (P.L. 118-31); and the Senator Elizabeth Dole 21st Century Veterans Healthcare and Benefits Improvement Act (P.L. 118-210, 2025; per [MC-11](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-11)). Foundational pro-claimant case law includes <em>Brown v. Gardner</em> 513 U.S. 115 (1994), <em>Henderson v. Shinseki</em> 562 U.S. 428 (2011), and <em>Kingdomware Technologies, Inc. v. United States</em> 579 U.S. 162 (2016) making the VA Vets First rule of two mandatory. The state layer threads through the Pennsylvania Military and Veterans Code (51 Pa.C.S.); 51 Pa.C.S. § 8904 (PA Disabled Veterans Real Estate Tax Exemption — full real estate tax elimination for 100% service-connected veterans, no income test); the PA Blind Veterans Pension; and PA <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#dmva">DMVA</a> at Fort Indiantown Gap. The Philadelphia layer threads through the Philadelphia Mayor's Office of Veterans Affairs; the City of Philadelphia Office of Veterans Affairs (<a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#cvso">CVSO</a>); the Philadelphia City Veterans Advisory Commission (<a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pcvac">PCVAC</a>). The principal federal facilities serving PA-3 are the Corporal Michael J. Crescenz VA Medical Center (<a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#cmcvamc">CMCVAMC</a>) at 3900 Woodland Ave., Philadelphia, PA 19104, and the Philadelphia VA Regional Office at 5000 Wissahickon Avenue, Philadelphia, PA 19144. For the analytical treatment of how each instrument operates and where its gaps fall, see the seven <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/">D24 sub-domain pages</a>.</p>

## Constitutional foundation

### U.S. Constitution

**Article I § 8 cls. 12-13 — War Powers** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/constitution/articlei#section8)).<br>
Congress's power to raise and support armies and to provide and maintain a navy authorizes federal provision for veterans as an incident of the war power. VA healthcare, disability compensation, pension, education benefits, home loan guaranty, employment programs, and contracting preferences all operate under federal plenary authority — a statutory scheme subject to congressional appropriations and executive administrative discretion at every level, not a constitutional entitlement or state-regulated system.<br>
*Cited in:* every D24 sub-domain.

**38 U.S.C. § 511 — Decisions of the Secretary; Finality** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/511)).<br>
Historic preclusion of judicial review of VA benefits decisions; modified by the Veterans' Judicial Review Act of 1988 (P.L. 100-527) creating the Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims. <em>Henderson v. Shinseki</em> 562 U.S. 428 (2011) confirms judicial review under modified architecture.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/appeals/">SD7 Appeals &amp; Adjudication</a>.

### Pennsylvania Constitution

**PA Constitution Article III § 32 — Veterans benefits and exemptions**.<br>
Constitutional authorization for the state veterans benefits architecture including the <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pa-disabled-veterans-exemption">PA Disabled Veterans Real Estate Tax Exemption</a> at 51 Pa.C.S. § 8904 (full real estate tax elimination for 100% service-connected veterans).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/access-pathway/">SD6 Access Pathway</a>; <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/housing-homelessness/">SD4 Housing</a>.

## Federal statutes — VA structure and Cabinet status

**Department of Veterans Affairs Act of 1988** (P.L. 100-527; [102 Stat. 2635](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/STATUTE-102/STATUTE-102-Pg2635)).<br>
Elevated the Veterans Administration to Cabinet-level Department effective March 15, 1989. Consolidated VHA (healthcare), <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#vba">VBA</a> (benefits), and NCA (memorial affairs) under unified Secretary-level oversight at 810 Vermont Avenue NW, Washington, DC 20420. Same act (as Veterans' Judicial Review Act provisions) created the Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims.<br>
*Cited in:* every D24 sub-domain.

**Title 38, United States Code** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38)).<br>
The consolidated organic statute for veterans benefits. Substantive organization: Part I (general), Part II (substantive benefits), Part III (readjustment and related benefits), Part IV (general administrative provisions), Part V (<a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#bva">BVA</a>, CAVC, claim procedure), Part VI (benefits oversight and information).<br>
*Cited in:* every D24 sub-domain.

## Federal statutes — VA Healthcare (SD1)

**Title 38 U.S.C. Chapter 17 — Hospital, Nursing Home, Domiciliary, and Medical Care** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/chapter-17)).<br>
Primary authority for VHA hospital care and medical services. § 1710 establishes basic entitlement to VHA care for veterans with service-connected disabilities and other priority categories. §§ 1703A-1703N (as amended by VA MISSION Act) govern the Veterans Community Care Program. § 1720D governs mental health care for military sexual trauma. Statutory stability HIGH; recent significant expansions through PACT Act (2022) and MISSION Act (2018).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vha/">SD1 VA Healthcare</a> (principal anchor).

**Title 38 U.S.C. Chapters 73-77 — VHA organizational authority**.<br>
Chapter 73 establishes the Veterans Health Administration as an organizational component of VA; Chapters 74-76 govern VHA staffing (Title 38 hybrid personnel system); Chapter 77 covers research programs.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vha/">SD1 VA Healthcare</a>.

**VA Maintaining Internal Systems and Strengthening Integrated Outside Networks Act of 2018 (VA MISSION Act)** ([P.L. 115-182](https://www.congress.gov/bill/115th-congress/senate-bill/2372); 132 Stat. 1393; codified at 38 U.S.C. § 1703 et seq.).<br>
Enacted June 6, 2018. Created the Veterans Community Care Program (VCCP) replacing the Veterans Choice Program. Eligibility criteria: 20-day wait threshold for primary care and mental health; 28-day threshold for specialty care; 30-minute drive time threshold. Final implementing regulations at 38 C.F.R. Part 17, Subpart G (84 FR 26278, June 5, 2019). <strong>GAO February 2026 finding (per [MC-04](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-04))</strong>: 9 of 27 GAO recommendations implemented, 1 closed, 17 remaining unimplemented (GAO-26-108943, March 4, 2026).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vha/">SD1 VA Healthcare</a> (principal anchor); [MC-04](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-04).

**Sergeant First Class Heath Robinson Honoring our Promise to Address Comprehensive Toxics (PACT) Act of 2022** ([P.L. 117-168](https://www.congress.gov/bill/117th-congress/senate-bill/3373); 136 Stat. 1759).<br>
Enacted August 10, 2022. Largest single expansion of VA health care and benefits eligibility in program history. §§ 101-104 expand VHA eligibility for toxic-exposure veterans (burn pits, Agent Orange, Gulf War-related); § 603 mandates toxic-exposure screening every five years; §§ 301-343 add 20+ presumptive conditions. Enrollment acceleration effective <strong>March 5, 2024</strong> — phased schedule eliminated.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vha/">SD1 VA Healthcare</a> (MC44); <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vba-disability/">SD2 VBA Disability</a> (MC44, [MC-07](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-07)).

**Senator Elizabeth Dole 21st Century Veterans Healthcare and Benefits Improvement Act** (P.L. 118-210, 2025).<br>
Expanded community care provisions; eliminated secondary-physician-review step in "best medical interest" community-care determinations effective May 2025; expanded caregiver support program access. Adds a new statutory layer above the MISSION Act community-care architecture (per [MC-11](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-11)).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vha/">SD1 VA Healthcare</a>; [MC-11](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-11).

**38 C.F.R. § 17.36 — Enrollment priority groups**.<br>
Eight groups in descending priority order; Priority Group 1 (service-connected disability ≥50% or unemployable) through Priority Group 8 (higher-income non-service-connected veterans with full copay obligations). Priority group assignment determines access sequencing and copay obligations.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vha/">SD1 VA Healthcare</a>.

## Federal statutes — VBA Disability, Pension &amp; VR&amp;E (SD2)

**Title 38 U.S.C. Chapter 11 — Compensation for Service-Connected Disability or Death** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/chapter-11)).<br>
Substantive entitlement to disability compensation for service-connected conditions. § 1110 entitlement; § 1114 monthly rates; § 1116 Agent Orange presumptive conditions; § 1117 Gulf War conditions. Statutory stability HIGH. <strong>2026 rates per [MC-03](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-03) (effective December 1, 2025; 2.8% [COLA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#cola))</strong>: 100% = $3,938.58/month; 90% = $2,362.30/month; 80% = $2,102.15/month; 70% = $1,808.45/month (no dependents).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vba-disability/">SD2 VBA Disability</a> (principal anchor); [MC-03](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-03).

**Title 38 U.S.C. Chapter 15 — Pension for Non-Service-Connected Disability or Death or for Service** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/chapter-15)).<br>
Income-based pension for wartime veterans with non-service-connected disability or low income. § 1521 wartime veteran pension; § 1541 surviving spouse pension. Aid-and-Attendance enhanced pension (38 U.S.C. § 1521(d), § 1541(d)) for veterans requiring assistance with activities of daily living or housebound.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vba-disability/">SD2 VBA Disability</a>.

**Title 38 U.S.C. Chapter 31 — Veteran Readiness and Employment (VR&amp;E)** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/chapter-31)).<br>
Vocational rehabilitation for veterans with service-connected disability ≥20%. Five rehabilitation tracks: reemployment; rapid employment; self-employment; employment through long-term services; independent living. 12-year time limit from later of separation date or date of notification of service-connected disability rating.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vba-disability/">SD2 VBA Disability</a>.

**38 U.S.C. § 5303 — Certain bars to benefits** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/5303)).<br>
The discharge characterization eligibility gate. A veteran discharged under dishonorable conditions is not eligible for most Title 38 benefits; certain misconduct discharges similarly bar entitlement. <strong>OTH discharges are subject to a "character of discharge" determination by VA (38 C.F.R. § 3.12)</strong> which may find the discharge "under conditions other than dishonorable" for benefits purposes. <strong>38 C.F.R. § 3.12(d)(3)</strong> provides special protection for OTH discharges shaped by military sexual trauma (<a href="/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#mst">MST</a>) and mental health conditions. <strong>MC45 substrate-formation</strong>: SD2 owns this analysis; cross-references appear at every other SD.<br>
*Cited in:* every D24 sub-domain; MC45.

**38 U.S.C. § 5107 — Claimant responsibility; benefit of the doubt** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/5107)).<br>
The pro-claimant benefit-of-the-doubt standard. When the evidence is in approximate balance, the benefit of the doubt is resolved in favor of the claimant. Substantively articulated through CAVC case law including <em>Gilbert v. Derwinski</em> 1 Vet. App. 49 (1990).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vba-disability/">SD2 VBA Disability</a>; <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/appeals/">SD7 Appeals</a>.

**38 U.S.C. § 5103A — Duty to assist claimants** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/5103A)).<br>
VA's duty to make reasonable efforts to assist a claimant in obtaining evidence necessary to substantiate the claim. Substantively articulated through CAVC case law.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vba-disability/">SD2 VBA Disability</a>; <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/appeals/">SD7 Appeals</a>.

## Federal statutes — VA Education Benefits (SD3)

**Post-9/11 Veterans Educational Assistance Act of 2008** ([P.L. 110-252](https://www.congress.gov/bill/110th-congress/house-bill/2642); codified at 38 U.S.C. § 3301 et seq.).<br>
Enacted June 30, 2008; effective August 1, 2009. Created Chapter 33 — full in-state public college tuition; monthly housing allowance at E-5-with-dependents BAH rate; annual books-and-supplies stipend. Transferability of unused entitlement to dependents.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/education-benefits/">SD3 VA Education Benefits</a> (principal anchor).

**38 U.S.C. § 3317 — Yellow Ribbon Program** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/3317)).<br>
Federal-institutional cost-sharing supplement to Chapter 33 for private institutions and out-of-state tuition above the annual federal cap. <strong>AY2025-26 private institution tuition cap $29,920.95; online-only MHA $1,169/month</strong>. PA-3 anchor institutions: Penn (unlimited contributions / unlimited slots, AY2024-25); Drexel (unlimited contributions / unlimited slots since 2009; all programs); Temple (unlimited contributions / unlimited undergraduate slots); Thomas Jefferson University (TJU) program participation. AY2025-26 Yellow Ribbon terms F-flagged (F24-SD3-01).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/education-benefits/">SD3 VA Education Benefits</a>.

**Harry W. Colmery Veterans Educational Assistance Act of 2017 (Forever GI Bill)** ([P.L. 115-48](https://www.congress.gov/bill/115th-congress/house-bill/3218)).<br>
Eliminated the 15-year time limit on Chapter 33 GI Bill use for veterans who separated on or after January 1, 2013. Made Purple Heart recipients fully eligible for Chapter 33 irrespective of length of service. STEM scholarship expansion.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/education-benefits/">SD3 VA Education Benefits</a>.

**Title 38 U.S.C. Chapter 35 — Survivors' and Dependents' Educational Assistance (DEA)** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/chapter-35)).<br>
DEA for surviving dependents of service-connected deceased veterans. No housing stipend; not Yellow Ribbon-eligible; flat monthly allowance structurally inferior to Post-9/11 GI Bill equivalent.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/education-benefits/">SD3 VA Education Benefits</a>.

## Federal statutes — VA Housing &amp; Veteran Homelessness (SD4)

**Title 38 U.S.C. Chapter 37 — Housing and Small Business Loans** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/chapter-37)).<br>
VA home loan guaranty (no down payment, no PMI). § 3702 basic entitlement; § 3703 guaranty amount; § 3710 use restrictions. Specially Adapted Housing (SAH) grants under § 2101 and Special Housing Adaptation (SHA) grants for severely disabled veterans.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/housing-homelessness/">SD4 Housing</a>.

**HUD-VASH** ([42 U.S.C. § 1437f(o)(19)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/1437f) + [38 U.S.C. § 8(b)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/8)).<br>
HUD allocates the housing-choice voucher; VA provides clinical case management; voucher and case management travel together to the veteran in a permanent supportive housing arrangement. <strong>P.L. 116-315 (2020) § 1011 mandates community-provider contracting when utilization falls below 85%</strong> — statutory recognition of the case-management capacity-constraint problem. <strong>Section 9103 of the FY2021 NDAA (P.L. 116-283)</strong> permanently extended HUD-VASH case-management eligibility to OTH-discharge veterans at risk of homelessness — the only D24 program substantively reaching across the MC45 substrate.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/housing-homelessness/">SD4 Housing</a>; [MC-05](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-05), [MC-10](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-10).

**Supportive Services for Veteran Families ([SSVF](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ssvf))** ([38 U.S.C. § 2044](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/2044)).<br>
Rapid rehousing for very-low-income veterans facing homelessness. Up to 4 months of rental assistance; outreach; case management. Administered through community-grantee partners.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/housing-homelessness/">SD4 Housing</a>.

**Grant and Per Diem ([GPD](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#gpd))** ([38 U.S.C. § 2011](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/2011)).<br>
Transitional housing for homeless veterans. Community-grantee operated bridge housing.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/housing-homelessness/">SD4 Housing</a>.

## Federal statutes — Veterans Employment &amp; SDVOSB (SD5)

**38 U.S.C. § 8127 — Small business concerns owned and controlled by veterans** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/8127)).<br>
The VA Vets First Contracting Program. <strong><em>Kingdomware Technologies, Inc. v. United States</em>, 579 U.S. 162 (2016)</strong>, made the VA Vets First "rule of two" mandatory, not discretionary, for VA contracting — a structural protection for SDVOSB opportunities at VA facilities including CMCVAMC.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/employment-sdvosb/">SD5 Employment &amp; SDVOSB</a> (principal anchor).

**15 U.S.C. § 657f — Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business contracting program** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/657f)).<br>
SBA SDVOSB set-aside authority. Government-wide SDVOSB contracting goal raised from <strong>3% to 5% by FY2024 NDAA (P.L. 118-31)</strong>. FY2023 actual: agencies awarded approximately $31.9 billion (~5%) to SDVOSBs at prime contract level. <strong>SBA VetCert</strong> under Section 862 FY2021 NDAA replaced the prior self-certification regime; backlog cleared November 2025; processing ~12 days.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/employment-sdvosb/">SD5 Employment &amp; SDVOSB</a>.

**38 U.S.C. § 4212 — Veterans' Employment Opportunities Act / VEVRAA** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/4212)).<br>
Federal-contractor affirmative-action obligation for protected veterans (disabled veterans; recently separated; active-duty wartime / campaign-badge veterans; Armed Forces service medal veterans). VETS-4212 annual reporting. <strong>Reporting threshold raised from $150,000 to $200,000 (per [MC-09](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-09))</strong>; all PA-3 anchor institutions remain subject. <strong>VEVRAA enforcement resumed July 2, 2025</strong> (D10 SD5 cross-reference). <strong>data.dol.gov public open-data portal launched February 18, 2026 (per [MC-09](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-09))</strong> made company-specific VETS-4212 protected-veteran-hiring data publicly accessible for the first time.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/employment-sdvosb/">SD5 Employment &amp; SDVOSB</a>; [MC-09](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-09).

**Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (WIOA) — Veterans' Priority of Service** ([38 U.S.C. § 4215](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/4215)).<br>
Veterans receive priority of service in all DOL-funded workforce programs delivered through CareerLink and similar one-stop centers.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/employment-sdvosb/">SD5 Employment &amp; SDVOSB</a>.

**Transition Assistance Program (TAP)** ([10 U.S.C. § 1142](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/10/1142)).<br>
DOD pre-separation transition program; coordinated with VA, DOL VETS, SBA. Provides employment workshop; benefits briefings; resource referral.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/employment-sdvosb/">SD5 Employment &amp; SDVOSB</a>.

## Federal statutes — Access Architecture &amp; Representation Pathway (SD6)

**38 U.S.C. § 5902 — Recognition of representatives of organizations** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/5902)).<br>
VA accreditation framework for representatives of approved Veterans Service Organizations (VSOs) including [DAV](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#dav), [VFW](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#vfw), American Legion, AMVETS, PVA. Accredited representatives may prepare, present, and prosecute claims at no cost to the veteran.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/access-pathway/">SD6 Access Pathway</a> (principal anchor).

**38 U.S.C. § 5904 — Recognition of agents and attorneys** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/5904)).<br>
VA accreditation framework for claims agents and attorneys. Fee agreements regulated; contingent-fee arrangements permitted post-decision under specified conditions.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/access-pathway/">SD6 Access Pathway</a>.

**38 U.S.C. § 5103 — Notice to claimants of required information and evidence** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/5103)).<br>
VA's duty-to-notify obligation. Pairs with § 5103A duty-to-assist.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/access-pathway/">SD6 Access Pathway</a>.

## Federal statutes — VA Appeals &amp; Adjudication (SD7)

**Veterans Appeals Improvement and Modernization Act of 2017 ([AMA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ama))** ([P.L. 115-55](https://www.congress.gov/bill/115th-congress/house-bill/2288)).<br>
Enacted August 16, 2017; effective February 19, 2019. Restructured VA appeals into three discrete review lanes — Higher-Level Review ([HLR](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#hlr); 38 U.S.C. § 5104B); Supplemental Claim with new and relevant evidence (38 U.S.C. § 5108); BVA appeal (Direct Review / Evidence Submission / Hearing). FY2025 final processing times per [MC-02](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-02): Direct Review ~506 days; Evidence Submission ~713 days; HLR ~60.7 days February 2026.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/appeals/">SD7 Appeals</a> (principal anchor); [MC-02](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-02).

**Title 38 U.S.C. Chapter 71 — Board of Veterans' Appeals** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/chapter-71)).<br>
BVA appellate authority for VA benefit decisions. § 7104 BVA jurisdiction; § 7107 docketing; § 7252 finality.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/appeals/">SD7 Appeals</a>.

**Title 38 U.S.C. Chapter 72 — Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/chapter-72)).<br>
Article I court of record reviewing BVA decisions. § 7251 court establishment; § 7261 scope of review (de novo legal review; clear-error factual standard). Further appeal to Federal Circuit (28 U.S.C. § 1295(a)(9)); Supreme Court certiorari available.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/appeals/">SD7 Appeals</a>.

**Equal Access to Justice Act (EAJA)** ([28 U.S.C. § 2412](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/28/2412)).<br>
Fee-shifting authority making attorney fees available to prevailing claimants in CAVC and Federal Circuit veterans-benefit cases against the federal government. Structural underwriting of veterans-benefits private-bar capacity.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/appeals/">SD7 Appeals</a>.

## Foundational case law

**<em>Brown v. Gardner</em>, 513 U.S. 115 (1994)** ([Supreme Court](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/513/115/)).<br>
The pro-claimant interpretive canon: ambiguities in statutes governing veterans benefits are to be construed in the veteran's favor. Articulates the substantive content of the benefit-of-the-doubt principle.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/appeals/">SD7 Appeals</a>; <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vba-disability/">SD2 VBA Disability</a>.

**<em>Henderson v. Shinseki</em>, 562 U.S. 428 (2011)** ([Supreme Court](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/562/428/)).<br>
The 120-day deadline to appeal a BVA decision to CAVC is not jurisdictional and is subject to equitable tolling. Confirms the pro-claimant interpretive frame extends to procedural deadlines.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/appeals/">SD7 Appeals</a>.

**<em>Kingdomware Technologies, Inc. v. United States</em>, 579 U.S. 162 (2016)** ([Supreme Court](https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/579/162/)).<br>
The VA Vets First "rule of two" at 38 U.S.C. § 8127 is mandatory, not discretionary — VA must set aside contracts for SDVOSBs and VOSBs whenever the contracting officer reasonably expects at least two such firms will submit qualified offers at a fair and reasonable price.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/employment-sdvosb/">SD5 Employment &amp; SDVOSB</a>.

## Federal statutes — cross-cutting recent legislation

**One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA)** ([P.L. 119-21](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/1)).<br>
Signed July 4, 2025. No direct VHA restructuring; core VA healthcare programs preserved. <strong>Indirect Medicaid work-requirement effects on ~1.75 million veterans on Medicaid</strong> (100%-disabled exempted); <strong>SNAP work-requirement changes affecting ~1.4 million veterans</strong>. OBBBA contributed to the 2025 federal government shutdown (43-day FY2026 spending deadlock); continuation appropriations maintained VA operations. SSVF and other discretionary supportive services experienced FY2026 funding uncertainty (per [MC-08](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-08)).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/vha/">SD1 VA Healthcare</a>; <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/veterans-affairs/sub-domains/housing-homelessness/">SD4 Housing</a>; [MC-08](https://github.com/square-party/square-party-site/blob/main/reference-info/verified-pa3-domain-content/D24-veterans-affairs/D24_vetAff_verified_2026-05-10.md#mc-08).
