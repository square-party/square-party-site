# Legal text appendix — Education

<p class="domain-lead">The legal chain that produces PA-3's educational architecture, organized constitutional → federal → state → local. The constitutional foundation runs through the U.S. Spending Clause for federal program authority, the 14th Amendment for civil-rights enforcement, and PA Const. Art. III § 14 (the Education Clause), which grounded the William Penn constitutional adequacy ruling. The federal substantive layer is statutorily stable; the state layer operates the substantive PA programs; the Philadelphia layer delivers the front-end administration. For the analytical treatment of how each instrument operates and where its gaps fall, see the seven <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/">D11 sub-domain pages</a>.</p>

## Constitutional foundation

### U.S. Constitution

**Article I, § 8 — Spending Clause** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/constitution/articlei#section8)).<br>
Authorizes federal financial assistance to state and local educational programs subject to federal program requirements. The constitutional basis for [ESSA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#essa) Title I, [IDEA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#idea), McKinney-Vento Title VII, [HEA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#hea) Title IV, Head Start, [CCDBG](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ccdbg), [WIOA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#wioa) Title II, and Perkins V. Conditional-spending principles articulated in *South Dakota v. Dole*, 483 U.S. 203 (1987), govern state-plan submission requirements.<br>
*Cited in:* every D11 sub-domain.

**14th Amendment, § 1 — Equal Protection / Due Process** ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/constitution/amendmentxiv)).<br>
Grounds the anti-discrimination architecture ([Title VI](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#title-vi), Title IX, Section 504) and provides the constitutional basis for judicial challenges to unequal educational conditions. [SFFA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#sffa) v. Harvard / UNC (2023) interprets equal protection to prohibit race-conscious admissions at institutions receiving federal financial assistance.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/postsecondary/">Postsecondary Architecture</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/specialized-populations/">Specialized Populations &amp; Civil Rights</a>.

### Key foundational cases

***Lau v. Nichols*, 414 U.S. 563 (1974)**.<br>
Held that failing to provide English language learner students with language access support constitutes national-origin discrimination under Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/specialized-populations/">Specialized Populations &amp; Civil Rights</a>.

***Davis v. Monroe County Board of Education*, 526 U.S. 629 (1999)**.<br>
Established the deliberate-indifference standard for student-on-student sexual harassment under Title IX.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/specialized-populations/">Specialized Populations &amp; Civil Rights</a>.

***Students for Fair Admissions v. Harvard / SFFA v. UNC*, 600 U.S. 181 (June 2023)**.<br>
Held that race-conscious admissions programs at Harvard and UNC violated the Equal Protection Clause — applicable to all institutions receiving federal financial assistance. First post-SFFA admissions cycle produced documented PA-3 enrollment shifts (Penn Black undergraduate enrollment 9.4% → 8.6%; Temple 20.9% → 29.7%).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/postsecondary/">Postsecondary Architecture</a>.

***William Penn School District v. Pennsylvania Department of Education***, Commonwealth Court of Pennsylvania, Judge Renée Cohn Jubelirer (February 7, 2023; final July 21, 2023).<br>
Held that Pennsylvania's school funding system is unconstitutional under both the Education Clause and equal protection grounds — the first Pennsylvania holding that education is a fundamental right under the state constitution. Documented an adequacy gap of approximately $4.6 to $6.2 billion. Load-bearing legal context for every PA state funding formula analysis throughout D11.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/operational-integration/">Operational Integration</a>.

### Pennsylvania Constitution

**Article III, § 14 — Education Clause** ([PA Constitution](https://www.legis.state.pa.us/cfdocs/legis/LI/consCheck.cfm?txtType=HTM&amp;ttl=00&amp;div=0&amp;chpt=3)).<br>
Directs the General Assembly to provide for a "thorough and efficient system of public education." Grounded the William Penn ruling.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a>.

**Article IX — Local Government** (including property-taxation authority).<br>
Governs the property-tax levy through which [SDP](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#sdp) receives the local share of its operating budget; SDP is the only school district in Pennsylvania without authority to levy its own taxes — 99% of SDP's operating budget is controlled by state and city officials.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a>.

## Federal statutory layer

### K-12 substantive

**Title I, Part A of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965 as amended by ESSA**, 20 U.S.C. § 6301 et seq. (P.L. 114-95, December 10, 2015) ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/chapter-70)).<br>
Principal federal grant program for K-12 public education; formula allocation to states based on number of low-income children; states sub-allocate to LEAs; LEAs target high-poverty schools. SDP receives among the largest Title I allocations of any single district. *Statutory stability: HIGH. Appropriation stability: VARIABLE.*<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/specialized-populations/">Specialized Populations &amp; Civil Rights</a>.

**Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA)**, 20 U.S.C. § 1400 et seq. ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/chapter-33)).<br>
Federal grant-and-entitlement statute governing special education. IDEA Part B for children ages 3-21 ([FAPE](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#fape); [LRE](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#lre); [IEP](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#iep); Child Find; procedural safeguards). IDEA Part C governs early intervention (birth through age 2). Implementing federal regulations at 34 C.F.R. Parts 300 and 303; PA implementing regulations at 22 Pa. Code Ch. 14. *Statutory stability: HIGH.*<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/early-childhood/">Early Childhood</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/specialized-populations/">Specialized Populations &amp; Civil Rights</a>.

**Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972**, 20 U.S.C. § 1681 ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/1681)).<br>
Prohibits sex discrimination in any education program receiving federal financial assistance. Implemented by ED [OCR](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ocr) at 34 C.F.R. Part 106. The Biden administration's 2024 Final Rule (substantially expanding protections including gender identity) was challenged in multiple federal courts; the 2020 Trump-era Rule remains the operational baseline in jurisdictions where the 2024 Rule is enjoined.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/postsecondary/">Postsecondary Architecture</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/specialized-populations/">Specialized Populations &amp; Civil Rights</a>.

**Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act of 1973**, 29 U.S.C. § 794 ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/794)).<br>
Prohibits disability discrimination in any program receiving federal financial assistance. In K-12 education, Section 504 operates alongside IDEA: students not IDEA-eligible may still qualify for Section 504 accommodations. K-12 implementation is more request-driven than IDEA's affirmative child-find obligation.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/postsecondary/">Postsecondary Architecture</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/specialized-populations/">Specialized Populations &amp; Civil Rights</a>.

**McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act, Title VII — Education for Homeless Children and Youth**, 42 U.S.C. § 11431 et seq. ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/chapter-119/subchapter-VI/part-B)).<br>
Requires states and LEAs to ensure homeless children have immediate enrollment in school (without prior school records, immunization documentation, or proof of residency); transportation to school of origin if in the student's best interest; and a local homeless liaison. The Title I (HUD/CoC) / Title VII (education) definitional gap means families in doubled-up housing situations are McKinney-Vento Title VII-eligible for educational services but Title I-excluded for housing services (MC19, carried forward from D7 SD6).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/specialized-populations/">Specialized Populations &amp; Civil Rights</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/operational-integration/">Operational Integration</a>.

**Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964**, 42 U.S.C. § 2000d ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/2000d)).<br>
Prohibits national-origin discrimination in programs receiving federal financial assistance; interpreted by ED OCR (after Lau v. Nichols) to require affirmative steps to provide meaningful language access to English language learners.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/specialized-populations/">Specialized Populations &amp; Civil Rights</a>.

**Equal Educational Opportunities Act (EEOA) of 1974**, 20 U.S.C. § 1703(f) ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/1703)).<br>
Requires school districts to take "appropriate action to overcome language barriers that impede equal participation by its students in its instructional programs."<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/specialized-populations/">Specialized Populations &amp; Civil Rights</a>.

**Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA)**, 20 U.S.C. § 1232g ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/1232g)).<br>
Governs student education records — access, disclosure, consent architecture.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/operational-integration/">Operational Integration</a>.

**ESSA Title IV, Part A — Student Support and Academic Enrichment (SSAE)**, 20 U.S.C. § 7111 ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/chapter-70/subchapter-IV)).<br>
Formula grants to states and LEAs for well-rounded education, safe/healthy learning environments, and effective technology use.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a>.

### Postsecondary

**Higher Education Act of 1965 as amended**, 20 U.S.C. § 1001 et seq. ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/chapter-28)).<br>
Principal federal statute governing postsecondary institutions. Title IV (20 U.S.C. § 1070 et seq.) governs student financial assistance: Pell Grant (20 U.S.C. § 1070a), William D. Ford Federal Direct Loan Program (20 U.S.C. § 1087a et seq.), [FAFSA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#fafsa) / SAI system (20 U.S.C. § 1090).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/postsecondary/">Postsecondary Architecture</a>.

**FAFSA Simplification Act**, P.L. 116-260 (2020). Operational for 2024-25 award year; the 2025-26 rollout moved to the GrantUS platform at [PHEAA](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pheaa).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/postsecondary/">Postsecondary Architecture</a>.

**Post-9/11 GI Bill / Yellow Ribbon Program**, 38 U.S.C. Chapter 33 ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/38/chapter-33)).<br>
Yellow Ribbon is the institutional-side commitment mechanism providing supplementary tuition funding beyond the GI Bill cap at private institutions. Penn, Temple, and Drexel participate at varying commitment levels. Cross-reference D24 Veterans Affairs for the institutional-side compliance architecture.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/postsecondary/">Postsecondary Architecture</a>.

**Gainful Employment Rule**, 34 C.F.R. Part 668. Promulgated by ED in July 2023 under the Biden administration; establishes program-level accountability for whether graduates' earnings and debt loads meet specified thresholds. Implementation and enforcement posture under the current federal administration is temporally variable.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/postsecondary/">Postsecondary Architecture</a>.

### Early childhood

**Head Start Act**, 42 U.S.C. § 9831 et seq. (originally Economic Opportunity Act of 1964; current authorization P.L. 110-134, 2007) ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/chapter-105/subchapter-II)).<br>
Direct federal grant program administered by HHS Administration for Children and Families (ACF) through local grantees. Comprehensive early childhood education, health, nutrition, and family support services to children birth through age 5 in families at or below FPL. Early Head Start serves families birth through age 3.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/early-childhood/">Early Childhood</a>.

**Child Care and Development Block Grant Act of 1990 as amended (CCDBG)**, 42 U.S.C. § 9858 et seq. (P.L. 113-186, reauthorized 2014) ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/chapter-105/subchapter-II-B)).<br>
Federal block-grant funding to states for child-care subsidies and quality-improvement activities. Subsidy / eligibility primary at <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/social-welfare/sub-domains/child-family-support/">D12 SD6 Child &amp; Family Support</a>; D11 SD3 notes CCDBG as authorizing the state child-care quality systems (Keystone STARS in PA).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/early-childhood/">Early Childhood</a>.

### Adult and career-technical

**WIOA Title II — Adult Education and Family Literacy Act (AEFLA)**, 29 U.S.C. § 3271 et seq. (Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act, P.L. 113-128, July 2014) ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/chapter-32)).<br>
Authorizes formula grants to states for adult education and literacy activities (ABE; ASE / [GED](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ged) preparation; ELA; [IET](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#iet); family literacy). Administering office: OCTAE. Boundary with WIOA Title I-B (workforce development): primary at <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/">D10 Labor &amp; Employment</a> SD7.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/adult-basic-education/">Adult Basic Education</a>.

**Strengthening Career and Technical Education for the 21st Century Act (Perkins V)**, 20 U.S.C. § 2301 et seq. (P.L. 115-224, July 2018) ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/chapter-44)).<br>
Authorizes formula grants to states for secondary and postsecondary CTE programs. Requires alignment between CTE programs and in-demand industry sectors; emphasizes work-based learning (internships; apprenticeships; cooperative education); requires equitable access for special populations.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/career-technical/">Career &amp; Technical Education</a>.

**National Apprenticeship Act**, 29 U.S.C. § 50 (1937) ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/29/50)).<br>
Authorizes DOL Office of Apprenticeship registration of apprenticeship programs. Registered apprenticeship primary at <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/labor-employment/">D10 Labor &amp; Employment</a> SD7; D11 SD6 engages the CTE-to-apprenticeship interface.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/career-technical/">Career &amp; Technical Education</a>.

### School facilities and operational

**Asbestos Hazard Emergency Response Act (AHERA)**, 15 U.S.C. § 2641 et seq. (Toxic Substances Control Act, Title II) ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/chapter-53/subchapter-II)).<br>
Requires LEAs to inspect school buildings for asbestos-containing materials, develop asbestos management plans, and take response actions. EPA administers; DOJ enforces criminal and civil violations through U.S. Attorney offices. The USAO-EDPA entered a multi-year Deferred Prosecution Agreement with SDP for AHERA violations (filed June 26, 2025); primary engagement at <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/physical-infrastructure/">D13 Physical Infrastructure</a> SD5.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/operational-integration/">Operational Integration</a>.

**Title X of the Housing and Community Development Act — Lead Paint Hazard Reduction**, 42 U.S.C. § 4851 et seq. ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/chapter-63A)).<br>
Governs lead-based paint hazard reduction; EPA administers through the Renovation, Repair, and Painting Rule at 40 C.F.R. Part 745. Approximately 225+ SDP buildings have lead paint per D13 verified material.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/operational-integration/">Operational Integration</a>.

**Healthy, Hunger-Free Kids Act of 2010 (HHFKA)** and the **National School Lunch Program / School Breakfast Program** ([USDA FNS](https://www.fns.usda.gov/cn)).<br>
Federal school nutrition architecture; primary engagement at <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/social-welfare/sub-domains/nutrition-assistance/">D12 SD3 Nutrition Assistance</a>. D11 SD7 engages the operational layer: the [Community Eligibility Provision (CEP)](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#cep) allows schools where at least 40% of students are directly certified to provide free meals to all students without individual applications. SDP participates at high proportion.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/operational-integration/">Operational Integration</a>.

**ADA Title II**, 42 U.S.C. § 12131 ([Cornell LII](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/42/12131)).<br>
Requires accessible transportation for students with disabilities; interfaces with IDEA LRE transportation obligations.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/operational-integration/">Operational Integration</a>.

### Federal agencies and oversight

**U.S. Department of Education (ED).** Administers the federal K-12 and postsecondary statutes through OESE (Title I), OSEP (IDEA), OPE / Federal Student Aid (HEA Title IV), OCTAE (WIOA Title II; Perkins V), and OCR (civil rights enforcement). Federal capacity reductions in 2025 affected operational consequence across the statutory floor.

**ED Office for Civil Rights (OCR).** Investigates complaints alleging violations of Title VI, Title IX, Section 504, and the Age Discrimination Act. The [Civil Rights Data Collection (CRDC)](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#crdc) is OCR's biennial data collection from public schools. 2025 enforcement collapse documented per GAO-26-108320 (January 2026) and HELP Committee Staff Report (April 2026); RIF rescinded December 2025 / January 2026; approximately 25,000 pending complaint backlog.

**HHS Administration for Children and Families (ACF).** Administers Head Start through the Office of Head Start. 2025 ACF reductions: approximately 30 to 45% RIF; 5 of 10 regional offices closed April 1, 2025 (Philadelphia remains open).

**DOL Office of Apprenticeship.** Registers apprenticeship programs and enforces non-discrimination obligations.

## State statutory layer

### K-12 substantive

**PA School Code of 1949**, 24 P.S. § 1-101 et seq.<br>
Comprehensive Pennsylvania statute governing public K-12 education. Board of Education governance; superintendent authority; teacher certification; mandatory attendance (24 P.S. § 13-1326); curriculum requirements; special education supplementary to IDEA; transportation obligations; state funding formula structure.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a>.

**PA State Funding Formula — Post-William Penn Architecture.** Distributes state Basic Education Funding (BEF) through a weighted-ADM formula. FY2024-25 PA budget added $1.1 billion for K-12 (largest single-year increase in state history) and $232 million more for Philadelphia schools. FY2025-26 PA budget (enacted November 12, 2025, 135 days late) added $565 million in adequacy-related funding. Cumulative investment approximately $1 billion against a $4.6 to $6.2 billion documented gap; approximately $3.8 billion remaining. Shapiro FY2026-27 proposal: additional $565 million statewide and $151 million more for Philadelphia.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a>.

**PA Charter School Law**, 24 P.S. § 17-1701-A et seq. (enacted 1997; amended multiple times).<br>
Authorizes establishment and operation of charter schools as independently operated public schools. Section 1725-A governs charter funding: SDP must pay each charter LEA the PDE Form 363 per-pupil tuition rate. Statutory mechanism producing the MC49 within-domain analytical-tension dynamic.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a>.

**PA Special Education Regulations**, 22 Pa. Code Ch. 14.<br>
PA's implementing regulations for IDEA Part B. Supplement the federal floor in several respects (additional procedural timelines; extended age range requirements; specific gifted education provisions without federal analog).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/specialized-populations/">Specialized Populations &amp; Civil Rights</a>.

**PA EITC PA (Educational Improvement Tax Credit)**, 72 P.S. § 8701-C et seq.<br>
Provides corporate tax credits for donations to Educational Improvement Organizations and Scholarship Organizations.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a>.

**[PA OSTC (Opportunity Scholarship Tax Credit)](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ostc)**, 72 P.S. § 8701-G et seq.<br>
Provides tax credits for donations to scholarship organizations serving students in "low-achieving" schools. Combined EITC PA + OSTC provided approximately $340 million in 2022-23 tax credits; FY2024-25 budget raised combined caps to $525 million annually. Fiscal-architecture analysis primary at <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/finance-taxation/">D9 Finance &amp; Taxation</a>.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a>.

### Postsecondary

**PA Community College Act**, 24 P.S. § 19-1901-A et seq.<br>
Governs Community College of Philadelphia (CCP) authorization. CCP is jointly sponsored by the City of Philadelphia and Philadelphia County; funded through tuition, City of Philadelphia appropriation, and Commonwealth community-college appropriation.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/postsecondary/">Postsecondary Architecture</a>.

**PA state-related institution architecture.** Pennsylvania designates four institutions as "state-related" — receiving annual state appropriations but operating as independent institutions: University of Pennsylvania, Temple University, University of Pittsburgh, and Lincoln University.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/postsecondary/">Postsecondary Architecture</a>.

**Pennsylvania Higher Education Assistance Agency (PHEAA).** PA's primary state student-aid agency. Administers the PA State Grant and the American Education Services (AES) federal loan-servicing portfolio.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/postsecondary/">Postsecondary Architecture</a>.

### Early childhood, adult, and CTE

**PA Pre-K Counts**, authorized under PA School Code (24 P.S. § 15-1533 et seq.) and annual appropriation acts; administered by the Pennsylvania Department of Education ([PDE](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#pde)).<br>
Funds high-quality part-day pre-K for three- and four-year-olds in families below 300% FPG; programs must be Keystone STAR 3 or STAR 4 rated to qualify.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/early-childhood/">Early Childhood</a>.

**PA Office of Child Development and Early Learning (OCDEL).** Jointly administered by PDE and PA DHS. Oversees the Keystone STARS quality improvement system, the Early Intervention program (IDEA Part C), and statewide CCDBG coordination.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/early-childhood/">Early Childhood</a>.

**PA Bureau of Adult Basic and Literacy Education (ABLE).** Within PDE; administers PA's WIOA Title II formula grant, operates the PALS state adult-education data system, and subgrants to local providers. Administers the HiSET / GED program through which PA residents obtain high school equivalency credentials.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/adult-basic-education/">Adult Basic Education</a>.

**PA Bureau of Career and Technical Education (Bureau of CTE).** Within PDE; administers Perkins V state plan; subgrants to PA career and technical centers (CTCs) and comprehensive high school CTE programs.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/career-technical/">Career &amp; Technical Education</a>.

### School safety, attendance, and discipline

**PA Safe Schools Act**, 24 P.S. § 13-1301-A et seq.<br>
Governs school safety, incident reporting, expulsion requirements, school safety plans, and school resource officer deployment. Creates mandatory reporting obligations for certain categories of school incidents to the PA Office of Safe Schools.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/operational-integration/">Operational Integration</a>.

**PA School Code mandatory attendance**, 24 P.S. § 13-1326.<br>
Pennsylvania compulsory attendance law (ages 8-17; until graduation or age 17). Truancy enforcement proceeds through school attendance officer intervention, referral to the District Justice for summary offense, and juvenile court referral for chronic truancy. Component mechanism in MC48 emergent-HOM inventory.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/operational-integration/">Operational Integration</a>.

## Local layer

### Philadelphia administrative architecture

**School District of Philadelphia (SDP).** PA first-class school district governed by a nine-member Board of Education (elected since 2017 under the Philadelphia Home Rule Charter amendment; previously mayoral-appointed); Superintendent Dr. Tony B. Watlington Sr. (appointed 2022). Approximately 330+ school buildings serving approximately 114,529 district school students (2024-25). Fifteen regional learning networks plus the Opportunity (alternative) network.<br>
*Cited in:* every D11 sub-domain.

**SDP Home Rule Charter** and **Pennsylvania Intergovernmental Cooperation Authority (PICA).** SDP cannot levy taxes independently and must negotiate its property-tax millage share through the city budget process. The Home Rule Charter and PICA architecture root SDP's unique loss of tax-levy authority — the only school district in Pennsylvania structurally constrained this way.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a>.

**SDP Office of Environmental Management and Services.** Administers SDP's AHERA compliance, lead-paint remediation, and facilities environmental management. Funded in part by Penn's $100 million donation (cross-reference <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/physical-infrastructure/">D13 Physical Infrastructure</a> SD5).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/operational-integration/">Operational Integration</a>.

**SDP Office of School Safety.** Administers SRO coordination, PBIS administration, threat assessment, and the SDP-PPD MOU governance.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/operational-integration/">Operational Integration</a>.

**SDP Office of Student Support Services.** Administers social-emotional learning coordination, school counselor coordination, McKinney-Vento liaison, and IDEA procedural compliance support.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/specialized-populations/">Specialized Populations &amp; Civil Rights</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/operational-integration/">Operational Integration</a>.

**SDP Office of Multilingual Curriculum and Programs.** Administers ELL identification (WIDA screener) and programming.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/specialized-populations/">Specialized Populations &amp; Civil Rights</a>.

**SDP-PPD Memorandum of Understanding.** Governs the deployment and authority of School Resource Officers in SDP schools. The mandatory referral provisions are load-bearing for the MC48 mechanism enumeration.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/operational-integration/">Operational Integration</a>.

### Other local institutional actors

**Charter LEAs operating in PA-3.** Approximately 63,964 brick-and-mortar charter students; 14,252 cyber charter students (October 1, 2024). Major operators: Mastery Charter Schools, KIPP Philadelphia, Universal Companies, String Theory Schools, Esperanza, Aspira. As of 2025-26, 17 Renaissance Charter Schools. Charter LEAs are separate LEAs for federal funding purposes.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a>.

**Archdiocese of Philadelphia.** Operates the Catholic school system as an independent educational authority. Approximately 16,000 Catholic school students in Philadelphia (down from approximately 24,000 in 2017-18).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/k12-architecture/">K-12 Substantive Architecture</a>.

**[PHLpreK](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#phlprek)** — administered by the City of Philadelphia Mayor's Office of Children and Families (OCF). Funded by the **Philadelphia Beverage Tax** (enacted 2017; 1.5 cents per ounce on sweetened beverages). 5,250 seats across 228 programs in 2024-25; universal application launched for 2025-26.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/early-childhood/">Early Childhood</a>.

**[Community College of Philadelphia (CCP)](/paul/campaign/empower/glossary/#ccp).** Sub-baccalaureate; approximately 21,000 students with approximately 60% Pell penetration; in-state tuition $8,688/year (2024-25; first tuition increase in nine years approved March 2026).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/postsecondary/">Postsecondary Architecture</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/adult-basic-education/">Adult Basic Education</a> · <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/career-technical/">Career &amp; Technical Education</a>.

**Community Learning Center (CLC).** Philadelphia's primary adult education provider, operated by SDP; serves adults in GED preparation, English literacy, and basic skills programs.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/adult-basic-education/">Adult Basic Education</a>.

**Free Library of Philadelphia.** Operates adult literacy programs as a WIOA Title II-funded local library adult education resource. Branch library network extends adult education access across all four PA-3 sub-areas.<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/adult-basic-education/">Adult Basic Education</a>.

**Anchor institutions in PA-3.** Penn (private Ivy; state-related; Yellow Ribbon participant); Temple (state-related; urban-serving research university); Drexel (private; co-op model; Drexel-Salus merger completed July 2025); Thomas Jefferson University (academic health system + university); Lincoln (state-related; nation's oldest HBCU; located in Chester County but serving significant PA-3 student population). Six-dimension anchor framework engagement: educational-institution role joins fiscal-authority constituent (D9), commerce-and-industry actor (D8), employer (D10), real-estate actor (D7), and healthcare-delivery actor (D21).<br>
*Cited in:* <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/education/sub-domains/postsecondary/">Postsecondary Architecture</a>.
