# Legal text appendix — Public Benefits & Safety Net

<p class="domain-lead">The legal chain that produces PA-3's public-benefits and safety-net architecture, organized constitutional → federal → state → local. This appendix is queued — the form mirrors the <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/law/finance-taxation/">D9 legal appendix</a>: each instrument cited with primary source link, plain-language summary, and cross-reference to the sub-domain pages where its operational treatment appears. The federal substantive layer (SSA Title XVI SSI, Title II SSDI, Title XIX Medicaid, Title XX SSBG; the Food Stamp Act / SNAP; Section 8 / McKinney-Vento; OAA Title III; ADA; Section 504 Rehabilitation Act; IDEA; Title IV-E foster care; OBBBA P.L. 119-21) is statutorily stable; the state layer (PA Human Services Code; PA Public Welfare Code; PA Code Title 55; the Working Pennsylvanians Tax Credit) operates the substantive PA programs; the Philadelphia layer (DHS code; PHA enabling architecture) delivers the front-end administration.</p>

<p>For the analytical treatment of how each instrument operates and where its gaps fall, see the <a href="/paul/campaign/empower/domain/social-welfare/sub-domains/">D12 sub-domain pages</a> (queued).</p>
