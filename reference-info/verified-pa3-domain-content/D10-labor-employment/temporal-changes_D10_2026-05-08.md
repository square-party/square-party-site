## TC-12: NLRB Joint Employer Rule Trajectory [P3-UPDATED]

**Claim anchor:** D10 SD4 §2 (MC-03 PRINCIPAL ANCHOR); D10 SD4 §7/§9.

| Date | Status / Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| September 30, 2015 | NLRB in *Browning-Ferris Industries*, 362 NLRB No. 186: broad joint employer standard adopted — entities jointly employ workers if each has authority to control essential terms, even if that control is indirect or reserved but unexercised. | *Browning-Ferris* (2015) |
| December 14, 2017 | NLRB in *Hy-Brand Industrial Contractors*, 365 NLRB No. 156: narrow standard restored — direct and immediate control required. | *Hy-Brand* (2017) |
| February 26, 2018 | *Hy-Brand* vacated by NLRB after ethics recusal issue; *Browning-Ferris* standard temporarily restored. | NLRB order (2018) |
| April 1, 2020 | Trump NLRB final rule: direct and immediate control standard formalized; *Browning-Ferris* standard superseded. | 85 Fed. Reg. 11184 |
| October 26, 2023 | Biden NLRB final rule: *Browning-Ferris*-type broad standard reinstated — indirect and reserved control is sufficient. | 88 Fed. Reg. 73946 |
| February 26, 2024 | *Chamber of Commerce v. NLRB*, E.D. Tex.: Biden 2023 rule vacated nationwide. | *Chamber of Commerce* (2024) |
| February 2026 | **Trump NLRB final rule: narrow "direct and immediate control" standard formally reinstated; Biden 2023 rule formally withdrawn.** Penn/Aramark joint-employer theory formally extinguished at the NLRB doctrinal level. | Source 13 |

**Vector summary:** Joint employer standard has oscillated four times in 11 years between broad (*Browning-Ferris*-type) and narrow (direct-and-immediate-control) standards. Current operative standard: narrow, formal rule as of February 2026.
