### TC-01 — MHPAEA Sept 2024 Final Rule formally paused

- **What was:** Sept 2024 Final Rule effective Sept 9, 2024; status under Trump 2 admin uncertain (possible enforcement rollback, modification, or delay) [G1-SD5-03 / F1 / F14]
- **What is now:** Tri-Agencies (DOL, HHS, Treasury) issued joint non-enforcement statement May 15, 2025. Will not enforce 2024 Final Rule's NEW provisions until ERIC litigation resolves + 18 months. 2013 Rule + CAA 2021 statutory NQTL comparative-analysis obligation remain in force. Rule under "reconsideration" — possible rescission or modification. EO 14219 (Deregulatory Initiative) is broader admin context.

- **Affects:** G1-SD5-01, G1-SD5-03, G1-SD5-05; F1, F14

### TC-02 — BSCA $1B school-MH funding freeze

- **What was:** Bipartisan Safer Communities Act (P.L. 117-159, June 2022) school-based MH funding flowing as ESSER-replacement bridge [G1-SD4-02]
- **What is now:** Trump admin froze $1 billion of BSCA school-MH funding in 2025; KFF documents federal grant share for school MH dropped from 53% (2021-22) to 33% (2024-25); 2,416 federal Department of Education employees lost jobs in 2025; 19 Community Schools 5-year grants terminated Dec 20, 2025

- **Affects:** G1-SD4-02, G1-XC-04 (federal-rep-leverage richness)

State-directed payment caps (~$149B). Work requirements + 6-month redeterminations effective Dec 30, 2026 (HHS extension authority to 2028). CBO: 11.8M lose coverage by 2034. House gender-affirming-care funding ban STRUCK by Senate Parliamentarian. IMD exclusion (42 U.S.C. § 1396d(a)(B)) UNCHANGED. Reentry continuity preserved (CAA 2023 § 5121 youth + CAA 2024 suspension-not-termination).

- **Affects:** G1-SD1-02, G1-SD3-01, G1-SD6-02, G1-XC-04; F4, F13

Resnick.

### TC-05 — NSH civil section permanently closed; forensic-only since 2019

- **What was:** NSH ~300 beds; civil §304 long-term commitments routed to NSH (geographic burden for Philadelphia families) [G1-SD2-06]
- **What is now:** NSH = 375 beds total, 100% forensic (255 Regional Forensic Psychiatric Center + 120 Forensic Stepdown). Civil section closed permanently January 2019 (Feb 2018 announcement). Civil §304 commitments from Philadelphia now route to Danville State Hospital (Montour County) — geographic burden EXACERBATED at different facility. NSH is the only remaining state psychiatric facility in southeastern PA. Forward-looking: Stantec + architecture+ designing new Southeast Psychiatric Treatment Center; initial 270 single-occupant beds expanding to 420 total; groundbreaking 2026.
- **When changed:** January 2019 (civil section closure; predates first-pass cycle but was inaccurately characterized in first-pass)
- **Affects:** G1-SD2-06 (long-term commitment family-contact externality now relates to Danville, not NSH); G1-SD6-01 (PPS-larger-than-NSH claim — holds at narrower margin: ~440-470 SMI vs 375 forensic NSH; future 420-bed expansion will narrow further); F5
